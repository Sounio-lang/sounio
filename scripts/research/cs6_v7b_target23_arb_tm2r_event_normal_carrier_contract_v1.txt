CS6 v7b target23 event-normal QR carrier contract v1

Purpose
- Replace the independent residual box at the frozen witness by a rational QR
  carrier designed for the event derivative D = x*y - w - ZS, where ZS is a
  frozen interval parameter rather than a state coordinate.
- Preserve the source variables xi and eta exactly as symbolic Taylor-model
  coordinates.
- Determine whether a full witness transport is justified before consuming a
  Foundry/Slurm allocation.

Carrier geometry
- The degree-2 Taylor model keeps xi, eta, rho0, rho1, rho2, and rho3.
- Four independent carrier coordinates sigma0 through sigma3 are appended; they
  never replace or alias an original Taylor-model variable.
- The event covector is c = (mid(y), mid(x), -1, 0).
- Column zero is a transported or newly seeded residual direction with c*q0
  nonzero.
- Columns one through three lie exactly in ker(c), checked with Fraction
  arithmetic.
- Doubleton transports q0 and completes the kernel.
- Tripleton transports q0 plus one dynamically selected kernel direction, then
  completes the kernel.
- The rational basis inverse is computed exactly.
- An event projection with exact w=0 switches to a three-dimensional carrier
  on physical rows (x,y,ell). Its section covector is (mid(y),mid(x),0), and w
  remains identically zero rather than being reconstructed from a coordinate
  hull that has discarded cancellation.

Rigorous enclosure
- Every interval coefficient radius, sigma-bearing monomial, and component
  remainder becomes a generator.
- Every monomial supported only on xi, eta, and rho0 through rho3 is retained
  in the primary Taylor model.
- Every generator is projected with Arb through the exact rational inverse.
- Coordinate radii are outward upper bounds of the projected generators.
- Q*(Q inverse*g) must contain every original generator.
- No point fallback and no box flattening are permitted.

Inductive normal form
- Immediately after every reconditioning, each component is the sum of a
  degree-2 polynomial in the six primary variables and four pure linear sigma
  terms, with zero component remainder.
- A flow step may create primary-sigma, sigma-sigma, or sigma-square monomials
  and a new interval remainder. Before the next step, every such term is ranged
  on the normalized variable cube and moved into the generator family.
- Odd or mixed monomials use their symmetric [-1,1] range. An all-even
  nonconstant monomial uses [0,1], represented as one-half constant plus one-half
  symmetric generator. Interval coefficient radii remain independent axis
  generators.
- The reconstructed carrier is an enclosure, not an algebraic identity and not
  a claim that dependencies discarded by the generator hull are recovered.
- The worker refuses unless every reconditioning restores this normal form; the
  verifier independently checks the serialized forms and exact check counts.

Preflight
- Post-hoc application to the frozen production and terminal endpoints is a
  negative control: it cannot recreate correlation already flattened into
  independent component remainders.
- Rebuild the exact witness subdomain from the hash-bound raw event projection,
  install the carrier before a new flow step, and compare one bounded step with
  the lineage-preserving representation.
- A mode becomes a full-transport candidate only if its one-step derivative
  width is smaller than the lineage-preserving control.
- The exact rational width margin must exceed the receipt verifier's 2^-230
  allowance for Arb endpoint rounding before the strict comparison is reported.
- The 18-times target remains the acceptance gate at the refused event tube.
- One-step improvement is not a whole-tube transversality certificate.

Transport
- Reconstruct the original frozen witness before enabling the new carrier.
- Continue the same TM2R transport with event-normal reconditioning after each
  accepted step.
- Preserve all six primary variables; sigma0 through sigma3 are the dynamic
  carrier coordinates and claim no original residual lineage.
- Symbolic-preservation acceptance ranges over exactly xi, eta, and rho0 through
  rho3. A zero dynamic carrier coordinate, including sigma3 in the anchored
  three-dimensional chart, is not loss of an original symbolic variable.
- Strict whole-tube positivity is required before interval Newton projection.
- Every section-anchored reconstruction is post-checked at exact rational zero
  width in w. An accepted transport must contain an event-local anchor in
  addition to the initial hash-bound section state.
- Reproducing the old lineage-carrier refusal is a historical control, not a
  prerequisite for a new carrier to succeed. A projection before that boundary
  may be promoted only when the complete upward Newton cover is accepted, every
  retained carrier has strictly positive tube derivative and projected normal,
  all six primary weights are present, and the receipt states explicitly that
  the frozen refusal was not reproduced.
- Every accepted first-section carrier object is retained in memory and
  continued with the same TM2R reconditioner to the next downward passage. A
  complete second passage requires one exact-w carrier per first-section leaf,
  strictly negative whole-slab derivative and projected normal, a nonzero
  downward-tube count, and represented dependence on all six primary variables.

Nonclaims
- Preflight alone certifies no section crossing.
- Neither mode is assumed to succeed.
- A second-section carrier is not by itself an h-set or a covering relation.
- No covering relation, recurrent graph, chaos, novelty, priority, or solution
  of an open problem follows from this experiment.
