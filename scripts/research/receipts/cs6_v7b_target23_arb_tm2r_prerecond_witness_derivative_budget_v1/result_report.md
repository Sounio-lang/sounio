# Witness derivative width budget result

## Result

The exact-rational diagnostic classified both frozen endpoint derivative models
as `DERIVATIVE_INTERVAL_REMAINDER_DOMINANT`.

At the terminal depth-18 endpoint, the reconstructed degree-2 model for
`D = u*v - w - z_s` encloses

`[-297.32166943797415, 340.8269847254264]`

with total width `638.1486541634006`. Its inherited interval remainder has
width `638.1389110524358`, or `99.99847322235952%` of the total.

The corresponding observed depth-18 time tube is kept separate from this
endpoint reconstruction. Its positive midpoint would require a radius
contraction greater than `14.668741391572123` to become strictly positive if
the midpoint were held fixed.

## Decision

The next transport experiment should recondition the transported `u` and `v`
remainder directions in a rigorous QR doubleton/tripleton carrier before
repeating the event test. Its quantitative acceptance target is to reduce the
combined `R_u * R_v`, `P_u * R_v`, and `P_v * R_u` contribution enough to make
the whole time-tube derivative strictly positive. A new symbolic split should
only be reconsidered after that reconditioning changes the remainder budget.
