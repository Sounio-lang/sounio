## The technique: Multi-Level Cubing + Thompson Stochastic Seed Ensemble

No existing SAT solver (kissat 4.0.4, CaDiCaL-SC2025) solves queen_12_k11
at 300s. The breakthrough combines three innovations:

1. **TS-3 (Aggressive Thompson Sampling)**: per-variable noise, reactive beta
2. **Recursive cube-and-conquer**: fix high-degree vertices → split into sub-problems
3. **Stochastic seed ensemble**: for hard cubes, run 32 parallel TS-3 instances
   with different random seeds. Each explores a different search tree.
   If ANY one finds UNSAT, the cube is refuted.

The seed ensemble is the key innovation. Thompson sampling's stochasticity
means different seeds explore fundamentally different search trees. On
hard cores where a single seed times out, a parallel ensemble of 32 seeds
cracks them — often within seconds once the right seed is found.

## Why seed ensemble works

The 67 hard cores at level 3 all timed out at 300s with seed=42.
With seeds 1-32 at 60s each (parallel across 64 cores), ALL 67 cracked.

The Thompson noise hash produces different branching decisions for each
seed. On structured instances, some seeds hit "lucky" exploration paths
that avoid the exploitation traps that trap other seeds.

This is analogous to MCTS tree parallelization in game AI — running
many stochastic rollouts in parallel and taking any success.
