M9 HAND PREDICTIONS -- written BEFORE the two-body master equation was solved
and BEFORE the M9 engine exists. Back-of-envelope Arrhenius arithmetic only.
Thermal relaxation of dissociated O pairs on Pd(100) after landing.

MODEL (assumptions, carried unchanged into the exact two-body solver and the engine)
  pair energies, O/Pd(100) pair-only fit (Zhang, Blum, Reuter, PRB 75, 235406
  (2007), as reported): nearest neighbour (1,0) +360 meV (repulsive), diagonal
  (1,1) +128 meV (repulsive), third neighbour (2,0) -48 meV (attractive);
  zero beyond. Many-body (trio) terms of the full fit are dropped.
  hops to empty nearest-neighbour hollows; barrier E = E0 + dE/2 (symmetric
  Bronsted-Evans-Polanyi, so rates obey detailed balance), E0 = 230 meV
  (Lin & Jiang 2023 quote this for O next to co-adsorbed O; using it for the
  isolated atom is an assumption), prefactor 1e12 /s.
  landing: final pair separation drawn from the Lin & Jiang Pd(100)
  histogram, or (4,0) for Bukas & Reuter; the whole dose lands at t = 0 onto
  empty sites; everything afterwards is thermal.

BARRIERS OF THE MOVES THAT MATTER (kT = 3.447 / 5.170 / 6.032 / 6.894 meV at 40 / 60 / 70 / 80 K;
rate = 1e12 exp(-E/kT) per channel; ln(1e12 x 1000 s) = 34.5 sets the
temperature at which a channel fires once in 1000 s: T* = E / (34.5 k))
  (1,0) -> (2,0)   dE = -408  E =  26   fires instantly at any T here
  (1,0) -> (1,1)   dE = -232  E = 114   loses to the above by exp(-88/kT) = e^-25.5 at 40 K
  (1,1) -> (2,1)   dE = -128  E = 166   4 channels, T* = 56 K; 60 K: 1.1e-2 /s each
  (2,1) -> (2,0)   dE =  -48  E = 206   2 channels, T* = 69 K; 60 K: 5e-6 /s, 70 K: 1.5e-3 /s, 80 K: 0.10 /s
  (3,0) -> (2,0)   dE =  -48  E = 206   2 channels, same numbers
  free hop         dE =    0  E = 230   T* = 77 K; 60 K: 5e-8 /s, 70 K: 2.8e-5 /s, 80 K: 3.3e-3 /s
  leaving (2,0)    dE =  +48  E = 254   6 channels, T* = 85 K; 80 K: 1.0e-4 /s each;
                                        after leaving, recapture ~0.9, so (2,0) is a trap

R = S_centre / theta = 2 P_same for each half-order field (the prediction card).
Parity classes by |D|^2 mod 4 contribute F0 / F1 = F2:  odd 0 / 1,  2 -> 2 / 0,  0 -> 2 / 2.
Starting values (histogram sums to 0.9944, normalised):
  Lin & Jiang: R(1/2,1/2) = 1.185, R(1/2,0) = R(0,1/2) = 1.383
  Bukas & Reuter (4,0): 2.000 / 2.000

PREDICTIONS, infinite dilution, hold 1000 s unless stated

 T1  40 K. Only (1,0) relaxes, always into (2,0): odd -> 0 mod 4.
       Lin & Jiang  R(1/2,1/2) = 1.185 + 2(0.0406) = 1.266
                    R(1/2,0)   = 1.383 + 1(0.0406) = 1.424
       (4,0)        2.000 / 2.000
 T2  60 K. Additionally (1,1) -> (2,1) (lifetime ~20 s): 2 mod 4 -> odd.
     (2,1) and (3,0) capture into (2,0) at 5e-6 /s: under 1% in 1000 s.
       Lin & Jiang  R(1/2,1/2) = 1.266 - 2(0.0380) = 1.190
                    R(1/2,0)   = 1.424 + 1(0.0380) = 1.462
       (4,0)        2.000 / 2.000 (0.0002 free hops per atom)
 T3  70 K. (2,1), (3,0) and the relaxed (1,1) pairs capture into (2,0)
     (95% within 1000 s): about 85% of Lin & Jiang pairs sit at (2,0).
       Lin & Jiang  R(1/2,1/2) ~ 1.9,  R(1/2,0) ~ 1.8
       (4,0)        ~0.2 free hops per pair; a hop into (3,0) is captured and
                    keeps the monopole, other hops flip it:
                    R(1/2,1/2) ~ 1 + exp(-2(0.22)(0.75)) ~ 1.7
     The two pictures come within ~0.2 of each other and their order inverts.
 T4  80 K. Lin & Jiang collapses into (2,0) within a minute (0.2 /s):
     R(1/2,1/2) ~ 1.9. (4,0) walks at 2.6e-2 /s (relative coordinate) until it
     reaches (3,0) or (2,1) and is trapped: R(1/2,1/2)(1000 s) = 1 + f_trap,
     f_trap between 0.3 and 0.7 (a first-passage number the solver will give).
     Order inverted: Lin & Jiang ABOVE (4,0).

CONSEQUENCE, if T1-T4 hold: the discriminating measurement has to be made at
<= 60 K (for a ~1000 s dose-and-measure cycle). The window is not set by free
diffusion (77 K) but by the attractive third-neighbour capture (69 K) --
i.e. by V(2,0) = -48 meV, the least certain number of the pair-only fit.
Above ~70 K the prediction inverts: a spot ratio near 2 would then no longer
indicate ballistic (4,0) separation.
