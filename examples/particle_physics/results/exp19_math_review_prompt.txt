Math review for particle EXP19 H Yukawa NonUnitary amplitude.

Formula in stdlib/particle_physics/nonunitary_amp.sio::h_bb_yukawa_amplitude_nu:
  y_b = sqrt(2) * m_b / v
  |M|^2 = y_b^4 * |D_H(s)|^2
where |D_H|^2 is the Breit-Wigner factor 1/[(s-M_H^2)^2 + (M_H Gamma_H)^2]
from nu_higgs_propagator, m_b from PDG MS-bar (GUM), v = higgs VEV ~ 246.22 GeV.

Cross-section leaf (EXP19, same as EXP16):
  sigma(s) = |M|^2(s) * s / (12*pi)
NWA peak toy (not equated): sigma_peak = 12*pi * Gamma_bb^2 / (M_H^2 * Gamma_H^2),
Gamma_bb = Br(H->bb)*Gamma_H.
