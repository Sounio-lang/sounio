# E252 - Reassociation strategy the multiplication law cannot certify

**Severity:** error

**Message pattern:** `error[E252]: algebra declares a reassociation strategy its multiplication law cannot certify: `free` needs an associative product, `fano_selective` needs the octonion level`

## What happened

An `algebra … { … reassociate: <strategy> }` declaration claims more product re-ordering
freedom than its declared multiplication law can back:

- `reassociate: free` with a non-associative product (`mul: alternative`,
  `non_associative`, or any `mul:` line without `associative`). Free re-association of a
  non-associative product changes values: on the octonions `(e₁e₂)e₄ − e₁(e₂e₄) = 2e₇`.
- `reassociate: fano_selective` on the sedenions or beyond. Fano selectivity is the
  octonion certificate (three basis supports inside one quaternionic Fano-line
  subalgebra); there is no such certificate at level ≥ 4.

A reassociation strategy is the type-level form of the **axis-1 certificate** of
anti-garbling (`formal/lean4/EpistemicEffectsNSA.lean`, `assocCert` / `reassoc_sound`):
certified re-association preserves value, true form and reported variance; an uncertified
one is an order-fabrication licence.

## How to fix

- Declare the law the algebra actually has and the strategy it certifies:
  associative → `free`; octonion-alternative → `fano_selective`; otherwise `blocked`.
- Or omit the clause: the checker derives the most permissive strategy the declared laws
  certify. (Before 2026-09-01 an omitted clause silently meant `free`.)
