# Above The Stars

> **Status**: Garden seed | **Last validated**: 2026-05-09 | **Source**: live session capture

## Butterfly

> butterflies are unsettled....garden needs to grow above the stars

The phrase arrived as a pressure change, not as a task title. The butterflies
are unsettled: the idea has energy, but has not landed in a proof, paper, gate,
or implementation yet. The Garden needs room above the stars: not away from
evidence, but high enough to hold the horizon before the evidence has a shape.

## Core Idea

Sounio needs a first-class Garden layer inside the repo: a place where early
ideas can be planted without being mistaken for validated research claims or
compiler milestones.

"Above the stars" names the layer above immediate gates. It is the place where
lightcone, eigenform, healing, self-hosting, 168, GPU proof ladders, and future
children can coexist as connected seeds. The point is not to make them public
claims. The point is to preserve their structure until the right executable
bridge appears.
