# Convergence Detector — same shape, independently arrived

> **Status**: Garden seed | **Last validated**: 2026-08-30 | **Source**: kimi-cli2 conversation on butterflies, following the Pharos seed (branch `lane/cursor-1/20260826`)

## Butterfly

> um detector de convergência — instrumento que avisa quando duas linhagens independentes têm a mesma forma — transformaria acidente feliz em método

The phrase arrived while listing new butterflies, when the conversation turned
to the one convergence the repo has already lived: the sedenion zero-divisor
census colliding with the crown-graph QEC code prediction — two lineages,
nobody planned the meeting. The founder's Garden protocol says it plainly:
*when butterflies are singing, look for convergence between several ideas*.
The Garden today is passive storage; this butterfly is the instrument that
would make "look for" executable.

## Core Idea

**Convergence = shared orbit under a declared gauge.** The template already
exists inside the repo: the Pireus operator-genesis bilinear v2 partitioned
65,536 matrices into exactly 32 equivalence classes under a declared symmetry
group (`GL(4,2)` basis changes, sign gauges, operand exchange). "Same shape"
there means "same orbit". The detector generalizes that move across domains:

- Each lineage produces artifacts with a **declared symmetry group** — the
  domain says what counts as "same". The gauge is declared, never discovered;
  otherwise the problem falls into general graph isomorphism and
  undecidability. Closed-world discipline: the instrument grows by growing the
  gauge inventory, not by pretending universality.
- The detector compares **invariants, not meanings** — adjacency spectra,
  defect-count vectors (Pireus classes already carry `squares`, `commutator`,
  `associator` fingerprints), order-dependence signatures. A fingerprint
  collision is a *lead*, not a theorem.
- Output is emitted at the `Hypothesis` evidence label, never higher. A
  candidate convergence must then be killed or confirmed by constructing the
  actual isomorphism witness. LLMs sit at `ROLE_REVIEW_ONLY`: no machine
  declares convergence; the instrument only points.

**Calibration witness (the instrument's first breath):** replay the known
convergence blind. Feed the detector the zero-divisor census artifacts and the
crown-graph QEC artifacts with their gauges declared, without revealing the
ending. If the canonical forms fail to collide, the instrument failed — a
missed known convergence falsifies it. Same design as the Pharos planted-crime
calibration: you calibrate a detector on an event you already know happened.

**Open question, fixed at planting:** what is a cross-domain "shape"? Inside
algebra, orbit under `GL(4,2)` is crisp; under what gauge could a clinical
pathway and a non-associative algebra collide? Working bet: order-dependence
spectra — what changes when the sequence is permuted. The associator is the
algebra's order fingerprint; a drug-administration pathway has its analogue.
If two such signatures collide under a declared gauge, the result is not the
metaphor "octonions are like pharmacology" but a witnessed isomorphism — or
nothing.

## What This Is Not

- Not similarity search, not embedding vibes, not an LLM reading the docs and
  finding "connections".
- Not an automated novelty engine; AM's fate (unverifiable claims, inflated
  hype) is the anti-goal, and every output is capped at `Hypothesis` until a
  witness exists.
- Not a claim that the zero-divisor ↔ QEC convergence generalizes to other
  domain pairs.
- Not a claim on general graph isomorphism; gauges are declared per domain.
- Not clinical guidance, not user-facing, not a statement about what Sounio
  computes today.

## Next Executable Bridge

The **blind calibration replay**: assemble the two artifact sets of the known
zero-divisor → crown-graph QEC convergence, declare their gauges, run the
canonical-form comparison without the ending revealed. The instrument passes
iff the two forms collide; a miss falsifies the fingerprint schema before any
new domain is admitted.
