## Required distinctions

```text
algebraic representation != biological ontology
inductive advantage       != causal mechanism
non-associative order     != proof of molecular non-associativity
classifier accuracy       != RNA folding accuracy
corruption recognition    != structure prediction
family-held-out           != clan-held-out unless the clan mapping proves it
single run                != replicated evidence
compile success           != empirical support
```

The exploratory KIMI result is preserved as hypothesis-generating evidence.
Its C0-C5 OctTree-versus-Clifford grid is an auxiliary test of a narrower
mechanistic question. An OctTree-Clifford tie may demote the claim that
non-associativity is necessary on that classifier, but it cannot set the
SedenTree, pseudoknot, or hierarchy-interaction outcomes. The confirmatory gate
may support, narrow, contradict, or refuse the original hierarchy hypothesis
without rewriting it.
