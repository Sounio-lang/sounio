# Proof-Carrying Reflexive Inquiry


Status: **executable**

Concept-ID: `SOUNIO-REFLEXIVE-INQUIRY`

## Founder Intent

Observation, questioning, instrumentation, and inference must not be treated as
automatically passive. When an inquiry may change a relation, an instrument, or
the observer's knowledge, its effect locus and order belong to the evidence.

## Executable Core

The D3 kernel represents an inquiry state with separate relational-process,
instrument, observer-version-space, and ordered-trace layers. A synthetic
action carries an effect footprint, but the footprint only raises an
interference obligation. Exact paired execution decides whether a declared
schedule commutes on state, trace, both, or neither.

Three frozen hypotheses share the same initial full state and observable
projection:

- passive observation;
- relational-process reactivity;
- instrument adaptation.

The relational and instrument hypotheses produce the same projected `P;Q`
and `Q;P` traces. The order effect therefore removes the passive hypothesis but
cannot identify its locus. A separately provenance-bound layer audit may
identify a locus only within the declared family.

## Ontology Binding

`stdlib/ontology/reflexive_inquiry.sio` distinguishes passive and reactive
inquiry, potential interference and exact non-commutation, admissible and
missing order traces, locus ambiguity, biological mechanism, real-person
counterfactuals, and clinical authority.

This is currently a parallel nominal boundary. The ontology module and its
negative witnesses independently re-express the kernel's distinctions, but a
runtime D3 receipt is not yet transported as an ontology-typed result. A
result-identity bridge requires separate source-to-IR evidence.

## Required Invariants

- Passive observation is distinct from reactive inquiry.
- Potential write-set overlap is not an exact non-commutation proof.
- Equal final state is not enough to reorder emitted evidence traces.
- Non-commutativity under reversed order is not non-associativity under changed
  parenthesization.
- A matched synthetic schedule pair is not a real-person counterfactual.
- An order effect does not identify whether the process, instrument, observer,
  or an omitted layer changed.
- Missing or unaudited trace evidence cannot eliminate a hypothesis.
- Provenance is checked before every version-space transition.
- The base-31 provenance fingerprint is an audit convenience, not a
  collision-free identity or authentication proof.
- Full-state expansion retains the order effect while making the frozen
  transition deterministic; it does not make the observable projection a
  sufficient state.
- Declared burden is not subjective suffering or measured harm.

## Claims Forbidden

- EMA, interviewing, diagnosis, or psychiatric observation is universally
  reactive or universally passive.
- The declared three-locus family is exhaustive in reality.
- A within-family locus identification is a causal or biological mechanism.
- A synthetic layer audit is a physical measurement.
- A projected order effect establishes octonionic or other algebraic
  non-associativity.
- A green gate authorizes diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, monitoring, or any
  clinical action.
- Sounio originated dynamic epistemic logic, effect systems, interactive
  POMDPs, measurement-reactivity research, or performative prediction.
