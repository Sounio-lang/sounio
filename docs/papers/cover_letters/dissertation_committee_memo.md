## Reading guide

The chapter is ~70 pages with the following structural map:

- §§ 1-2: Setting and background. Skim if you have read the prior chapters.
- §§ 3-5: PL substrate (composition, p-box operator, refinement types). Detailed but compact; matches the PL paper draft.
- §§ 6-7: Vancomycin case study and empirical evaluation. Self-contained; matches the clinical paper draft.
- §§ 8-9: Discussion and synthesis with adjacent chapters (rapamycin BBB, surgical interventions). New material; would value committee feedback here.
- § 10: Future work.

## Companion submissions

Two journal manuscripts are in flight from the same M1-M4 work:

1. POPL 2027 (PL paper — formal substrate)
2. *Clinical Pharmacokinetics* (clinical validation paper)

The chapter is the "best of both"; if the journal submissions land successfully in 2027 the chapter is the unifying thesis-level argument.

## Open questions for committee

I would value committee guidance on:

1. **Lean discharge depth** for the chapter. The PL paper is happy to leave the probabilistic obligation explicitly deferred; the dissertation may want to discharge it more completely (8-12 weeks of additional Lean work). Acceptable to defer to a follow-up committee deliverable?

2. **Cohort scope**. The current plan uses a single-center retrospective cohort. The committee may prefer a MIMIC-IV-only validation (fully open, reproducible) or a multi-center extension (stronger external validity).

3. **Synthesis with the rapamycin BBB chapter**. Section 9 currently presents the Sounio epistemic substrate as the unifier. Is the committee comfortable with this framing, or would they prefer a more incremental cross-reference?

## Timeline

I plan to address committee feedback over weeks 1-4 post-feedback and re-submit by end of week 6 for defence scheduling.
