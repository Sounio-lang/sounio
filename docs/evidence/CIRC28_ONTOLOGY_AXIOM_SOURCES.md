# Evidence for stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_ontology.sio

Every axiom below was read from a source opened on 2026-11-15 (DailyMed label XML through the DailyMed services API, the FDA table page, PubMed abstracts through NCBI E-utilities). Each quoted phrase (under 15 words) was checked by program to occur verbatim, after whitespace normalisation, in the saved source text. Axiom numbers A* (route clears victim) and P* (perpetrator acts on route) are the ones in the comments of `circ28_ax_table`.

Conventions: the FDA table and the labels speak of "CYP3A"; the concept used here is CYP3A4 (id 1). "clears" = major route of elimination. Strength classes follow the FDA table: strong = AUC of a sensitive substrate up 5-fold or more (or down by 80 percent or more for inducers), moderate = 2- to under 5-fold (down 50 to under 80 percent). Weak inhibitors and inducers are not encoded.

## Not sourced (left out)

- CYP2C9 (enzyme id 5): no concept. No sourced substrate among the 15 drugs and no sourced perpetrator edge needed it (rifampicin is a moderate CYP2C9 inducer in the FDA footnote, no victim).
- Tacrolimus as a P-gp substrate: the Prograf label text opened has no P-gp statement. Not encoded.
- Haloperidol glucuronidation (the largest share of its hepatic clearance, PMID 10628896) and haloperidol CYP2D6: the UGT isoform is not named in the abstract, and the CYP2D6 statement there is contradictory. Not encoded.
- Olanzapine CYP2D6 (label: minor in vivo), venlafaxine CYP3A4 (not a major route), risperidone N-dealkylation (no enzyme named): not encoded as major routes.
- Itraconazole and clarithromycin as P-gp inhibitors: the FDA footnote gives "Inhibitor of P-gp" with no strength class, and no strength role fits it. Not encoded; they act on CYP3A4 only.
- Fluvoxamine weak CYP2D6 inhibition; UGT induction by carbamazepine or rifampicin; any other weak or moderate perpetrator edge: not encoded (no opened source states a route-level fact usable here).
- Perpetrator-versus-perpetrator interactions (for example rifampicin lowering ketoconazole) and any perpetrator outside the eight: not encoded.
- Semaglutide and tirzepatide as perpetrators (labels: very low or low potential to inhibit or induce CYP enzymes): no perpetrator edge.
- Renal-handling interactions of lithium and vancomycin: no perpetrator in the list acts on the renal route, so no pair; a renal-handling perpetrator would need its own source and a renal perpetrator role.
- Magnitude of any interaction (AUC ratio), dose, time course: not represented.
