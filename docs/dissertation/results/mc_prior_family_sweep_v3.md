# PBPK28 Prior-Family Sweep — v3 (mass-conserving kernel)

**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Replaces:** `mc_prior_family_sweep_v2.md` (and v1).
**Harness:** `stdlib/darwin_pbpk/validation/pbpk28_mc_prior_family_sweep.sio`.
**Configuration:** rapamycin 5 mg IV bolus, N = 2000, seed = 1729, 0–168 h; MC dt = 0.5 h,
GUM dt = 0.05 h, Hessian dt = 0.1 h.
**Why v3:** v2's MC, GUM and Hessian all stepped with `pbpk28_full_cn_step`, whose negativity
floors injected mass. At the nominal parameters (audit) that inflated AUC_blood by +140% at
dt = 0.5 h, +52% at 0.1 h and +28% at 0.05 h, and the amount varied across samples, so each
family's spread was also distorted. All paths now use the Rannacher-started clamp-free
Crank–Nicolson of `theta_pbpk28.sio` (`docs/audit/PBPK28_CN_RANNACHER_MASS_BALANCE_2026-09-26.md`).
**Engine:** lean_single (Madaros aborts with `rc=182` before and after the fix).
**Run logs:** `runs/mc_prior_family_sweep_v3.txt` (legacy prior, the v2 configuration),
`runs/mc_prior_family_sweep_m6_v1.txt` (M6 prior).
**Convention:** rel_X = |u_X − u_MC| / u_MC (computed from unrounded values); u in mg·h/L. The
module's gate is rel_Hess ≤ 0.10 for at least one family (`MC_PRIOR_FAMILY_SWEEP_PASS`); other
families are reported, not gated.

## Findings (measured)

- Under the legacy prior, LogNormal (0.0155) and TruncNormal (0.0139) meet the Hessian gate.
  Under the M6 prior, LogNormal (0.0188) meets it and TruncNormal (0.279) does not. Positive
  Gaussian fails under both.
- No family meets the 5% first-order gate under either prior (rel_GUM ≥ 0.134).
- The TruncNormal/LogNormal ordering reverses between the two priors (legacy: TruncNormal
  u_MC 0.322 < LogNormal 0.332; M6: TruncNormal 0.288 > LogNormal 0.212). The reason was not
  analysed.
- Limits as in `mc_cross_validation_lognormal_v3.md`: MC resolution ≈ 1.6%, a 12.4 vs 12.7 L/h
  linearisation-centre mismatch, and different dt grids.

## Dissertation wording (§4.13)

**Safe to cite:** "On the mass-conserving integrator, the LogNormal prior meets the ≤ 10% Hessian
criterion under both hepatic-clearance priors (rel_Hess 0.0155 at σ/CL = 0.58, 0.0188 at 0.38).
The TruncNormal prior meets it at 0.58 (0.0139) but not at 0.38 (0.279). No family meets the 5%
first-order criterion." Add the limits sentence from `mc_cross_validation_lognormal_v3.md`.

**Withdrawn (v2):** "No prior family passes the Hessian criterion"; "the model is in a strongly
nonlinear regime driven by CL_hep CV = 58%"; "reducing CV below ~20% via Bayesian posterior
updating would be required to achieve convergence". The v1 → v2 `ms28_exp` Taylor-bug correction
stands; it is independent of this change.
