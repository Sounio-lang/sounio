# Functor F — G₂-covariance across the seven Fano lines

**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Status:** `HYPOTHESIS` → `EXECUTABLE` (target)  
**Parents:** `docs/research/functor_f_jet_functoriality_spec_2026-07-25.md` (F_GREEN), `docs/research/rupture-r4-fano-field_2026-07-25.md` (R4_GREEN)  
**Harness:** `scripts/research/functor_f_g2_covariance_contract.py`  
**Gate:** `scripts/ci/functor_f_g2_covariance_gate.sh`

---

## 1. What this is

The base Functor F contract verified the jet-functorial assignment on a single worked Fano line `(1,2,3)`. This extension asks: **does the assignment behave the same way on every Fano line?**

In octonion geometry, the 7 Fano lines correspond to the 7 quaternion subalgebras of 𝕆 generated by pairs of imaginary basis units (𝕆 has uncountably many quaternion subalgebras; these are the basis-aligned ones). The automorphism group G₂ acts transitively on the set of all quaternion subalgebras. A genuine functor F should not depend on which basis-aligned line we happen to write down first. This contract tests that **uniformity across the 7 basis-aligned lines** — a necessary condition for G₂-covariance — without constructing the full group action.

---

## 2. Claim

For every Fano line `L` and every off-line direction `u`:

1. The associator jet is pure and single-axis: `α(ε) = ±2ε · e_m`.
2. Φ_fp gives `a(ε) = -1 + ε²` and `b(ε) = τ ± ε`.
3. The same path classes (contrariety C and contradiction D) appear under the same dials.
4. The Betti-0 witness drops from 2 to 1 across the fold.

If these hold uniformly, Functor F is **line-independent**; the choice of worked line in R3 was a convenience, not a special case.

> **Scope of this contract (refined 2026-07-25).** The uniformity proved here is the
> `e_m = e_argmax` restriction — to the finite subgroup of signed basis permutations —
> of a stronger statement. Under a *generic* or *continuous* automorphism `g ∈ G₂`,
> Φ_fp's `b` extracted by coordinate `argmax` is **not** covariant (it sign-flips the
> selected pole); the natural pairing `b := ⟨α, e_m⟩` is. That obstruction and its fix
> are executed in `functor_f_g2_equivariance_contract.py` (`H_CHARACTERISED`) and
> `functor_f_phi_fp_equivariant_contract.py` (`E_GREEN`). `phi_fp` here now carries an
> optional `ref_axis` so the equivariant `b` can be evaluated off the basis-aligned
> lines; the default reproduces the on-basis `argmax` exactly, so every clause below is
> unchanged.

## 3. Contract clauses

| Clause | Statement | Acceptance gate |
|---|---|---|
| **G1_UNIFORM_JET** | For every Fano line and every off-line unit, `\|α(1)\| = 2.0` and `α` is supported on exactly one imaginary axis. | `PASS` if all 7×4 off-line jets satisfy the bound. |
| **G2_UNIFORM_PHI** | For every Fano line, `a(ε) = -1 + ε²` and `b(ε) = τ ± ε` up to axis relabeling. | Numeric agreement to `1e-9` for all lines. |
| **G3_PATH_C_UNIFORM** | For every Fano line, Path C (`τ = -α_m/2`) ends in a neutral well at `ε = 2`. | All 7 lines report `end_neutral=True`. |
| **G4_PATH_D_UNIFORM** | For every Fano line, Path D (`τ = 0`) ends in a polar well and sign-flips with `ε → -ε`. | All 7 lines report `polar_flip=True`. |
| **G5_BETTI_UNIFORM** | For every Fano line, the Betti-0 witness drops from 2 to 1 across the fold. | All 7 lines report `betti_drop=True`. |
| **G6_CROSS_LINE_CONSISTENT** | For at least one pair of intersecting Fano lines, the cross-line jet produces the same uniform path classes. | Pair `(1,2,3)` and `(1,4,5)` passes. |

---

## 4. What this is NOT

- **Not a construction of G₂.** We do not enumerate `Aut(𝕆)` or verify the group law.
- **Not pointwise naturality.** We do not prove `F(g·L) = g·F(L)` for all `g ∈ G₂`; we verify numerical uniformity across the orbit.
- **Not D3.** No identity with the ZD variety or with Petitot's full stratification.
- **Not a clinical claim.**
