# Categorical Semantics for `Knowledge<T>`: A Restricted Giry-Kleisli View, with Quantum-Markov Lift to Jordan Algebras and Sedenion Collapse

**Status**: Theory appendix, Sounio paper. Sketch — not a closed proof.
**Author scope**: Sounio core; intended to be reviewed by category theorists and operator-algebraists before submission.
**Epistemic tags**: Each substantive claim is annotated `[CANONICAL]`, `[DEFENSIBLE]`, `[CONJECTURAL]`, or `[CONSULT]`.

---

## 0. Thesis

`Knowledge<T>` in Sounio is, up to finite-moment truncation, an object in the Kleisli category of a *restricted* Giry monad. For `T` a real, possibly non-associative algebra, the monad lifts the algebraic multiplication via pushforward of product measures. For Jordan-algebra-valued `T` (in particular `Herm_3(O)`, the Albert algebra), the structure refines to a quantum Markov category in the sense of Parzygnat (2020). For sedenion-valued `T`, the lift fails on the zero-divisor locus, and this failure is the categorical content of Sounio's *confidence-collapse* compile-time event.

The aim is not to settle this picture but to expose the structure clearly enough that (i) Sounio's epistemic semantics inherit a citable foundation where one exists, and (ii) the genuinely open problems are visible.

---

## 1. The Classical Giry Monad

Let `Meas` denote the category of measurable spaces and measurable maps. The **Giry monad** `G: Meas → Meas` (Giry 1982 [LNM 915]) is defined on objects by

```
G(X) = { probability measures on X }
```

equipped with the σ-algebra generated by evaluation maps `ev_A: P ↦ P(A)` for measurable `A ⊆ X`. On morphisms, `G(f)(P) = f_*P`, the pushforward.

The **unit** is the Dirac embedding

```
η_X : X → G(X),    x ↦ δ_x.                                         [CANONICAL]
```

The **multiplication** is integration of measures over measures:

```
μ_X : G(G(X)) → G(X),    Q ↦ ( A ↦ ∫_{G(X)} P(A) dQ(P) ).           [CANONICAL]
```

Verification of the monad laws is in Giry (1982); a textbook account is in Panangaden (1999) [ENTCS 22, "The category of Markov kernels"].

A **Markov kernel** `X ⇝ Y` is a measurable map `X → G(Y)`. Markov kernels are exactly the morphisms of the Kleisli category `Kl(G)`; composition is the Chapman–Kolmogorov integral. On Polish spaces this is the standard category of probability theory; on standard Borel spaces it is the canonical setting for Bayesian inference (Cho–Jacobs 2019; Fritz 2020).

For our purposes the salient fact is that Kl(G) is **strongly symmetric monoidal** with monoidal product `(X, Y) ↦ X × Y` and tensor of kernels via product measures — when the kernels are independent.

## 2. `Knowledge<T>` as a Restricted Kleisli Arrow

### 2.1 The Sounio object

Sounio's runtime `Knowledge<T>` carries:

- `mean: T`,
- `variance: Σ` (a positive bilinear form on the tangent space of `T`),
- `dof: ν` (effective degrees of freedom, Welch–Satterthwaite),
- `confidence: c ∈ [0, 1000]` (integer-quantised),
- `provenance: ProvenanceRef`.

Forget the provenance and the integer quantisation — they are bookkeeping. The remaining triple `(m, Σ, ν)` is precisely the data extracted by truncating a probability measure on `T` to its **first two moments plus an effective sample size**, in the spirit of GUM Supplement 1 (JCGM 101:2008) and the Welch–Satterthwaite construction.

### 2.2 The truncation functor

Let `KGeneral` denote the Kleisli category `Kl(G)` restricted to objects that are real Polish vector spaces (or smooth manifolds with a chosen connection — needed for variance transport on non-flat `T`). Define a **truncation functor**

```
τ : KGeneral → K
```

acting on objects by `τ(X) = X` and on morphisms `f: X ⇝ Y` (a Markov kernel) by

```
τ(f)(x) = ( m_f(x), Σ_f(x), ν_f(x) ),
```

the first two moments of the kernel `f(x)` together with an effective DoF computed by Welch–Satterthwaite. Sounio's category `K` has these moment triples as morphisms; composition is

```
(g ∘_K f)(x) = τ( g ∘_{Kl(G)} lift(f) )(x),
```

where `lift` chooses a representative kernel with the prescribed moments — typically Gaussian, by maximum-entropy. **`K` is not a category in the strict sense**: composition is associative *only* up to higher-moment error. It is a category on the Gaussian slice `K_Gauss` ⊂ `K`. [DEFENSIBLE]

### 2.3 Composition is GUM-S2

Concretely, if `f` and `g` are linear with Gaussian noise, `τ(g ∘ f)` is exact and reproduces ordinary covariance propagation `Σ_g∘f = J_g Σ_f J_g^T + Σ_g`. For nonlinear `f, g`, `τ` introduces a first-order error coinciding with **GUM Supplement 2 (JCGM 102:2011)** uncertainty propagation. The categorical statement: **the truncation functor is exact on the linear-Gaussian slice and first-order on the smooth slice.** [DEFENSIBLE; folklore in the probabilistic-numerics literature, e.g. Hennig–Osborne–Girolami 2015]

### 2.4 What this buys

- `Knowledge<T>` values are objects `T` with a distinguished morphism `1 ⇝ T` in `K`.
- `with Epistemic` blocks correspond to lifting a pure computation `T₁ → T₂` to a Kleisli arrow `T₁ ⇝ T₂` in `K`.
- The compile-time error `E170` (knowledge `.value` requires `with Epistemic`) is the categorical assertion that one cannot extract a `T` from `T ⇝ T` without choosing a section — and Sounio insists the section be marked.

## 3. Lift to Algebraic Structure on `T`

### 3.1 Pushforward of multiplication

Let `T` be a real algebra (associative, alternative, Jordan, or merely bilinear) with multiplication `μ_T: T × T → T`, assumed measurable (automatic for finite-dimensional `T`). Define on `G(T) × G(T) → G(T)`:

```
(α, β) ↦ (μ_T)_*(α ⊗ β).                                            [CANONICAL]
```

Here `α ⊗ β` is the product measure (independence assumption), and `(μ_T)_*` is the pushforward along the multiplication. This is the standard construction of a **probabilistic algebra structure**.

### 3.2 Properties preserved under pushforward

| Property of `μ_T`         | Inherited by `(μ_T)_*`?                          |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Bilinearity               | Yes (on independent factors)        [CANONICAL] |
| Commutativity             | Yes                                  [CANONICAL] |
| Associativity             | Yes (when associativity holds in `T`) [CANONICAL] |
| Alternativity             | Yes on the diagonal of independent triples [DEFENSIBLE] |
| Power-associativity       | Yes                                  [DEFENSIBLE] |
| Existence of zero divisors | **Yes — and pathologically (§5)**   [CANONICAL] |

### 3.3 Truncated version

Restricting along `τ`, the operation on `K`-objects becomes:

```
(m_α, Σ_α) ⊗ (m_β, Σ_β)
   ↦ ( μ_T(m_α, m_β),  J_L Σ_α J_L^T + J_R Σ_β J_R^T ),
```

where `J_L = ∂μ_T/∂a |_{(m_α, m_β)}` and `J_R = ∂μ_T/∂b |_{(m_α, m_β)}`. This is **GUM-S2 for algebra products**; it is what `Knowledge<H>` and `Knowledge<O>` actually compute in Sounio. [CANONICAL — this is just bilinear error propagation]

### 3.4 The associator becomes a morphism

For non-associative `T` (e.g. octonions), define the **associator kernel**

```
[·,·,·] : T × T × T → T,    (a, b, c) ↦ (ab)c - a(bc).
```

Pushing forward independent triples gives a morphism in `K_Gauss`:

```
A_K : K(T) ⊗ K(T) ⊗ K(T) → K(T),
A_K(α, β, γ) = ( [m_α, m_β, m_γ],  J_assoc · diag(Σ_α, Σ_β, Σ_γ) · J_assoc^T ).
```

For octonions, the mean is the algebraic associator (zero on associative subspaces, non-zero on Fano triples). The variance is generically non-zero — the *associator carries epistemic content even when its mean vanishes*. This is the categorical correlate of the Fano-selective rewrite rule already in Sounio's e-graph (commit `eeb3747a`). [CONJECTURAL — needs explicit calculation on a few Fano triples]

## 4. Quantum Markov Categories and Jordan Algebras

### 4.1 Parzygnat's framework

Parzygnat (2020), "Inverses, disintegrations, and Bayesian inversion in quantum Markov categories", extends Markov categories (Fritz 2020; Cho–Jacobs 2019) by replacing the classical probability monad with a **CP-map monad** on (finite-dim) C*-algebras. Objects are C*-algebras; morphisms are completely positive unital maps (Schrödinger picture: trace-preserving CP maps on states). The category retains a comonoid structure on each object (copy + delete) only on the commutative subalgebra — quantum information cannot be cloned, encoding the no-cloning theorem categorically.

### 4.2 Jordan algebras as the "real-quantum" home

Jordan algebras (Jordan–von Neumann–Wigner 1934; Hanche-Olsen–Størmer 1984) generalise the self-adjoint part of a C*-algebra. The **JBW-algebras** (Jordan Banach von Neumann algebras) admit a fully developed probability theory: states are positive unital functionals; conditional expectations exist on JW-subalgebras; spectral resolutions hold (Alfsen–Shultz 2001). The exceptional Jordan algebra `Herm_3(O)` (the Albert algebra) is the unique finite-dim JBW-algebra not arising as the self-adjoint part of an associative algebra.

### 4.3 The proposed lift

**Conjecture [CONJECTURAL, requires CONSULT with Parzygnat or Westerbaan]**. There exists a Markov-style category `JMark` whose:

- objects are JBW-algebras,
- morphisms are normal Jordan-positive unital maps,
- monoidal product is a chosen Jordan tensor (delicate: the tensor product of JBW-algebras is *not* canonically a JBW-algebra; cf. Hanche-Olsen 1985 on the failure of Jordan tensor products for the Albert algebra),
- comonoid structure exists exactly on the associative (= classical or standard-quantum) subalgebras.

If `JMark` exists with the expected properties, then `Knowledge<Herm_3(O)>` lives in its truncated-Kleisli analogue, and Sounio's `with Epistemic` semantics on Albert-algebra-valued observables inherit categorical foundations from Alfsen–Shultz state theory.

### 4.4 What is *not* claimed

- That `JMark` already exists in the literature in a form ready to plug in. The closest is Westerbaan–Westerbaan–van de Wetering on effectus theory and the Cho–Jacobs–Westerbaan synthetic-probability programme; none of them treats the exceptional Albert algebra explicitly. [CONSULT]
- That the strong-Markov property (Panangaden's conditional independence) lifts. The non-distributivity of the Albert lattice of projections is a known obstruction; whether the categorical conditional-independence axiom of Cho–Jacobs survives is open. [CONJECTURAL]
- That `Knowledge<O>` (raw octonions, not Hermitian octonion matrices) is in `JMark` at all. It is not — `O` is non-Jordan. The intended home for raw octonion knowledge is the Albert algebra via the Jordan envelope `a ∘ b = (ab + ba)/2`. [DEFENSIBLE]

### 4.5 Practical reading for Sounio

`Knowledge<Octonion<f64>>` operations decompose into:

1. **Algebraic mean**: octonion product (bilinear, alternative).
2. **Variance propagation**: Jacobian-based GUM-S2 in the underlying R^8.
3. **Categorical envelope**: when expectations are taken (e.g. `expectation_value` of a Hermitian observable), the result naturally lands in the Albert algebra and then in `JMark`.

The compiler need not know this; the type system needs only to enforce that scalar expectations are extracted via the Jordan envelope when the user requests "quantum probability" semantics.

## 5. Sedenion Collapse — Failure of the Lift

### 5.1 The arithmetic obstruction

Sedenions `S = R^16` (Cayley–Dickson on `O`) are non-alternative, non-Jordan, and *contain zero divisors* (Moreno 1998; Khalil–Yiu 1997). Specifically, there is a 5-dimensional real-algebraic variety `Z ⊂ S × S` such that

```
(a, b) ∈ Z  ⟺  ab = 0  with  a ≠ 0 and b ≠ 0.                       [CANONICAL]
```

Moreno characterised `Z` as the set of pairs of *primitive zero divisors* arising from doubled-quaternionic embeddings.

### 5.2 The pushforward pathology

Let `α, β` be measures on `S` whose supports overlap `Z`. Then

```
(μ_S)_*(α ⊗ β)
```

has positive mass at `0 ∈ S` even when `α({0}) = β({0}) = 0`. **The product measure is pushed into a delta concentrate at zero on the zero-divisor sheet.** [CANONICAL — direct from the change-of-variables formula, since `μ_S^{-1}({0}) ∩ supp(α ⊗ β)` is a positive-measure set]

In moment-truncated form: `m_{αβ} → 0` while `Σ_{αβ}` does *not* shrink to zero. The signal-to-noise ratio diverges; the effective DoF becomes ill-defined; the integer-quantised confidence collapses to the floor.

### 5.3 The categorical statement

The map

```
G(S) ⊗ G(S) → G(S),  (α, β) ↦ (μ_S)_*(α ⊗ β)
```

is *bilinear* and *measurable*, but **not continuous** in the weak topology near the zero-divisor stratum: a sequence of pairs `(α_n, β_n)` whose supports approach `Z` produces a discontinuous jump in the variance of the result. Hence the lift `μ_S` to the truncated Kleisli category `K` is well-defined pointwise but discontinuous as a `K`-morphism. [DEFENSIBLE — standard pushforward-discontinuity argument; deserves a full proof in any final paper]

In `JMark` terms: `S` is not a Jordan algebra (the Jordan identity fails), so it is not an object of `JMark` at all. The categorical home for `Knowledge<Sedenion<f64>>` is therefore *coarser* than `JMark` — perhaps the category of measurable groupoids with partial composition (cf. Lawvere–Rosebrugh on category theory of partiality), or a Markov category over a relaxed positivity. **No off-the-shelf categorical home is currently identified.** [CONSULT]

### 5.4 Compiler manifestation

Sounio already encodes this at the type level. The multiplication operator on `Knowledge<Sedenion<f64>>` should carry a refinement predicate

```
type SafeProduct = { (a, b): Sedenion × Sedenion | dist((a,b), Z) > δ_user }
```

and `require_confidence(a*b, c)` should fail to type-check when the algebraic context cannot certify `dist(., Z) > δ`. The verified runtime check `confidence < c_threshold ⇒ trap` realises the same constraint dynamically. In categorical terms, the compiler is *refusing to construct a `K`-arrow that is provably discontinuous at its evaluation point*.

This is, to our knowledge, the first compile-time deployment of a zero-divisor-aware confidence calculus. [CONJECTURAL until lit. survey is closed; CONSULT with Baez et al.]

## 6. Open Questions and Research Agenda

1. **Truncation–monad compatibility.** Does `τ : KGeneral → K` factor as `τ = U ∘ T` for some submonad `T` of `G`, with `U` a forgetful functor? On the Gaussian slice, yes (the Gaussian Kleisli category is a known sub-Markov-category of `Kl(G)`; Fritz–Perrone 2020). In general, almost certainly no — moment truncation is lossy and convolutions of non-Gaussians escape any finite-moment slice. [CONJECTURAL]
2. **A native Jordan-Giry monad.** Is there a monad `G_J` on JBW-algebras that reduces to `G` on the commutative-associative subcategory? Westerbaan's effectus-theoretic state monad is a candidate; whether it factors through JBW for the exceptional case is open. [CONSULT]
3. **Fano-selective rewrite as natural transformation.** The Sounio compiler's rewrite `(ab)c → a(bc)` selectively on associative triples *is* a natural transformation on the subcategory of associative-triple morphisms. The challenge is to extend it to a *partial* natural transformation on `K(O)` whose domain of definition is a recognisable predicate. [DEFENSIBLE; promising tractable problem]
4. **Sedenion home.** What is the right ambient category for `Knowledge<Sedenion<f64>>`? Candidates: (i) Markov categories with partial morphisms (Di Lavore–Román 2022); (ii) effectus theory with sub-states; (iii) measurable groupoids. None is a clear match. [CONSULT with Westerbaan, Fritz, Parzygnat]
5. **Higher-moment Sounio.** If Sounio later admits skewness/kurtosis, the truncation functor refines to a `n`-th-moment functor. Does the resulting tower stabilise? Compare to the Edgeworth expansion as a tower of Markov-category enrichments. [CONJECTURAL]

None of these blocks Sounio's current operational semantics. They are the work that turns the present sketch into a theorem.
