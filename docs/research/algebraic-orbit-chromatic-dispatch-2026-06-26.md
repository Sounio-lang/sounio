## Algebraic angles (from de Grey 2018, arXiv:1804.02385)

The field is generated by rotation angles ω_t = arccos(1 − 1/2t):
- ω₁ → √3/2 (60°)
- ω₃ → √11/6 (arccos 5/6)
- ω₄ → √15/8 (arccos 7/8) → needs √5
- ω₁₆ → √63/32 (arccos 31/32) → needs √7

Unit-distance graphs are formed by points at these angles. de Grey combined spindle gadgets (Moser spindle uses √3,√11) at these angles to reach χ=5.
