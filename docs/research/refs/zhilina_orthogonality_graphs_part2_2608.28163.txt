Abstract
We consider zero divisors of an arbitrary real Cayley–Dickson algebra such that
their components are both standard basis elements. We construct inductively the or-
thogonality graph on these elements. Then we show that, if we restrict our attention
to at least 16-dimensional algebras, two algebras are isomorphic if and only if their
graphs are isomorphic. We also provide an algorithm to retrieve the Cayley–Dickson
parameters of an algebra from its graph.
Keywords: Cayley–Dickson algebras, standard basis elements, orthogonality graphs,
graph isomorphism, algebra isomorphism.

components are both alternative elements of the previous algebra. This notion was

Proof. It follows from Theorem 4.6 that every nonspecial element A belongs to a hexagon
