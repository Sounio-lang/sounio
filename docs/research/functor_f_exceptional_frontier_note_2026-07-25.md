# Functor F and the exceptional series — a boundary note (what is *not* claimed)

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Orthography:** EN-UK
**Status:** `BOUNDARY NOTE` — draws the scope line at `G₂`; asserts **no** exceptional-series result
**Context:** `petitot-semantic-potential.md §4` ("the deep conjecture", flagged interpretive frontier + landmine), `rupture-programme-synthesis §1` ("what is NOT the contribution")

---

## 0. Why this note exists

The Petitot thread (`petitot-semantic-potential.md §4`) points at a **conjecture**: that
Wildgen's four-actant semantics requires the exceptional singularities `E₆, E₇, E₈`, that
octonions generate the exceptional Lie groups (`G₂ → F₄ → E₆ → E₇ → E₈`, Freudenthal–Tits),
and therefore that "the organizing centre of rich semantic morphology is the octonionic
exceptional structure". That doc labels this **not a result** — an interpretive frontier
with a live **landmine** (analogy mistaken for theorem). This note fixes the boundary for
the Functor-F programme so the arc cannot be read as crossing it.

---

## 1. The concrete facts (textbook, stated, not rediscovered)

- `G₂ = Aut(𝕆)`, `dim 14`, is the base of the octonionic exceptional tower.
- Above it, via the Freudenthal–Tits magic square over `𝕆`: `F₄` (`dim 52`,
  `= Aut(J₃(𝕆))`, the Albert algebra), `E₆` (`78`), `E₇` (`133`), `E₈` (`248`).
- The exceptional *singularities* `E₆/E₇/E₈` (Arnold) share ADE/Dynkin labels with the
  exceptional *Lie algebras* via the McKay correspondence.

These are standard mathematics, cited to place the programme, not claimed as findings.

---

## 2. Where the Functor-F programme actually lives — `G₂` only

Every executable rung of the Functor-F programme is `G₂`-representation-theoretic and
uses **nothing above `G₂`**:

- The forms it instruments are the `G₂` 3-form `φ` and 4-form `ψ` (`functor_f_g2_coherence`,
  `functor_f_g2_tower_closure`), verified `G₂`-invariant.
- Its symmetry is `Aut(𝕆) = G₂` and its finite Fano subgroup `PSL(2,7)`
  (`functor_f_fano_psl27_thread`).
- The sedenion/trigintaduonion ZD geometry it threads is acted on by (diagonal) `G₂` /
  `PSL(2,7)` — again base-level.

**No rung constructs, uses, or reaches `F₄`, `E₆`, `E₇`, or `E₈`.** The Albert algebra
`J₃(𝕆)`, the magic square, and the exceptional singularities do **not** appear in any
contract. The programme is *complete at the base of the tower*.

---

## 3. What is quarantined (forbidden overclaim)

- **Not claimed:** that the exceptional groups `F₄/E₆/E₇/E₈` are realised by, required by,
  or derivable from anything in this programme.
- **Not claimed:** the semantic conjecture — that four-actant morphology *is* the octonionic
  exceptional structure, or that the ADE label-match is a semantic theorem. Per
  `petitot-semantic-potential §4` this is an analogy; treating it as a result is the named
  landmine. It stays quarantined, in the same class as the `D3` prohibition ("`X ≡ ZD/associator/Petitot`").
- **Not claimed:** any bridge from `PSL(2,7)` to an exceptional Weyl group. `PSL(2,7)` here
  is `Aut(Fano) = GL(3,2)`, the symmetry of the octonion 3-form; no `E`-series role is
  asserted.

---

## 4. The one honest, testable statement this note licenses

*If* the exceptional-semantic conjecture is ever pursued, the entry point is the **first
place the Functor-F objects would have to leave `G₂`** — i.e. exhibit a `φ`/`ψ`/ZD structure
that is `F₄`-natural but not `G₂`-natural (e.g. on `J₃(𝕆)` rather than `𝕆`). No such object
exists in this programme; producing one — with the same executable, verdict-before-computing
discipline, and the same refusal to read a dimension/label coincidence as a bridge — is the
*only* honest way the exceptional frontier becomes more than an analogy. This note asserts
that step has **not** been taken.
