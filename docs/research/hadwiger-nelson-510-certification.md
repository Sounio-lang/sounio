# HeuleGraph510 — exact unit-distance certification (Phase B, COMPLETE)

**Status:** COMPLETE. Every edge of the smallest known 5-chromatic unit-distance graph
is certified at *exact* unit distance in its number field, with the converse audit, by
Lean `native_decide`. χ ≥ 5 itself remains Phase C (the UNSAT certificate).

## Data (authoritative)
- `vtx/510.vtx` (510 symbolic vertices) and `edge/510.edge` (DIMACS, 2504 edges) from
  Marijn Heule's CNP-SAT repository: https://github.com/marijnheule/CNP-SAT

## Field (asserted from the data, not assumed)
Radicands present in 510.vtx are exactly `{3,5,11,15,33,55,165}` ⇒ the realised field is
the **degree-8** `K = ℚ(√3,√5,√11)`. **√7 is absent** — de Grey's original G needs √7
(the L-assembly rotation 2·arcsin(1/8)), but Heule's SAT trimming dropped every
√7-bearing vertex. The data adjudicated the field; this is the audit that guards against
silent mis-measurement over a wrong field.
