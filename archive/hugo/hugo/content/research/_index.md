## Research Highlights

### Epistemic Computing Foundation

Sounio introduces a novel approach to uncertainty quantification in programming languages, with:

- **25+ citations** from peer-reviewed venues
- **GUM compliance** (ISO/IEC Guide 98-3:2008)
- **First implementation** of Knowledge<T> with provenance tracking
- **GPU-accelerated validation** for mathematical identities

### Octonion Neural Networks

Research on using 8-dimensional octonion algebra for neural networks:

- **8× parameter compression** compared to real-valued networks
- **120 FLOPs per multiply** with full uncertainty propagation
- **7/7 Moufang identity validation** with ε = 1e-6 tolerance
