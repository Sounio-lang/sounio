## what this receipt does NOT prove
This receipt proves the installed ELF is a self-reproducing fixed point of the recorded source bytes (g_k == g_{k+1}, self-repro). Provenance (ELF came FROM this source via this chain) is what the generation table + input_seed hashes are for; a future gate should require a SeedReceipt, not only the self-repro equality.
