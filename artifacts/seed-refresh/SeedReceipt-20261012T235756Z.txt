## what this receipt does NOT prove
This receipt proves the installed ELF is a self-reproducing fixed point of the recorded source bytes (g_k == g_{k+1}, self-repro). It does NOT by itself prove the ELF was *derived from* that source rather than substituted from another generation that happens to self-reproduce the same source. canonical_compiler_gate.sh checks md5(committed)==md5(self-compile) — stability, not provenance.
