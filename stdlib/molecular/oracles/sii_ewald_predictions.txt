P1 Ice rules: every oxygen donates exactly 2 and accepts exactly 2; 272 bonds per cell.
P2 Zero polarisation: the bond-winding vector W (integers) reaches (0,0,0) exactly,
   or the oracle reports the parity obstruction (then a 2x2x2 cell is required).
   Residual charge dipole of the SPC/E host |M| < 0.5 e A per cell (bisector geometry,
   bond lengths 2.76-2.80 A), against 136 x 0.489 = 66.5 e A if all were aligned.
P3 Ewald energy of a neutral H2 is independent of alpha in {0.25, 0.30, 0.35} to 1e-9 relative.
P4 Ewald guest-host energy equals the cubic direct sum of host-cell images (N -> large)
   to better than 1e-3 relative (neutral guest: the potential constant cancels;
   guest has no dipole: no surface term).
P5 Point-quadrupole limit: E_3site = 1/2 Q_ab d_a d_b phi with Q_ab = 2 q (d/2)^2 n_a n_b,
   agreement within 5 % at cage centres (hexadecapole correction O(d^4)).
P6 Laplace: the unweighted orientation average of E_3site at any charge-free point is
   0 up to the hexadecapole term: |<E>_Omega| < 2 % of the orientational spread.
   Consequence (Jensen): <exp(-E/kT)>_Omega >= exp(-<E>/kT) ~ 1, so classical charges can
   only RAISE the Langmuir constant relative to the uncharged centre model.
P7 Orientational spread (max - min E over directions) at the 24 cage centres:
   between 0.1 and 3 kJ/mol, wider in the small cages (closer waters), and it varies
   from cage to cage because the proton disorder differs cage to cage.
P8 Guest-guest Ewald for a set of H2 in the cell equals the absolutely convergent direct
   image sum (quadrupole lattice) to better than 1e-3 relative, alpha-independent to 1e-9.
