molecular::langmuir (stdlib port of sections 4g-4i). Written 2026-11-17 before the library, the oracle
(stdlib/molecular/oracles/langmuir_oracle.cpp) or the test were written.

The library: xorshift128+ normals (Box-Muller on molecular::lj's mol_uniform), Levy bridges, rejection draws of
x0 ~ exp(-w/T) in one cage, the path-integral ratio C_q/C_cl, the quantum pair ratios R_2, R_3, and the
charged-H2 ratio R_c (exact three-site Alavi energy in the Ewald potential of an ice-rule SPC/E host,
Gauss-Legendre x Fibonacci quadrature). The oracle mirrors the algorithms and the random stream in C++.

L1 (mirror) every number the test prints from a random stream (draws, bridge beads, ratios at small N)
   equals the oracle's to <= 1e-9 relative; deterministic quadratures (R_c) to <= 1e-9 relative.
L2 (independent consistency) at the test's small sizes, each statistical estimate agrees with the
   large independent runs of 4g-4i (different generator, different x0 sampler) within 4 of its own
   standard errors: ln(C_cl/C_q) SPC/E 250 K small cage 0.0800, large 0.0464; ln(R_2^cl / R_2^q) SPC/E 250 K
   0.155; ln R_c (seed 20261116) cage 0 = 0.01814 and cage 16 = 0.00918 (from exact.log) up to the
   coarser quadrature, which may move them by <= 10 %.
L3 (structure) R_c >= 1 for both cages; C_q < C_cl; R_2^q < R_2^cl.
