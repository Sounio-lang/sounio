Madaros: ADR-016 fusion of measured values. `meet(a, b, ...)` combines 2 to 8 `Knowledge<f64>` values by generalised least squares over their GUM covariance, so sources that share a component (a calibrant, force-field parameters) are weighed as correlated; the result carries the sources' channels at first and second order. It refuses at run time, with the sources and the statistic, when two sources are inconsistent above the `E_n` policy (default 1 at `k = 2`, `consistency: <literal>` to declare another, E305 if it is not a literal) and, for every policy, when a GLS weight is negative (the fusion would lie outside the sources). `meet_birge(...)` expands by the Birge ratio instead; `en_of`, `birge_of` and `neff_of` (1ᵀR⁻¹1) return the statistics. `contest(a, b, ...)` carries disagreeing sources as `Contest<f64>`, which is never used as a value, and `retract(c, x, because: "...")` is its only exit (E306 without a reason, E307 for a value that is not a source). Proofs: `formal/EpistemicCorr.lean` (soundness, refusals, symmetry, monotonicity, and that ignoring a positive correlation can only accept more). Oracles: `tests/oracles/epistemic_meet/` (C++23). Witness: `examples/molecular/h2_sii_water_contest.sio`, three water models of the sII hydrate that share the H₂ parameters: n_eff = 1.004, and meet refuses where independent treatment would report 32.41 ± 0.41.
