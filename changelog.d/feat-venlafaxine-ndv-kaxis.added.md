Users of the PBPK28 stdlib can now predict tissue:plasma partition coefficients (Kp) for ten of the fourteen organs with three published methods, Rodgers and Rowland, Schmitt (raw and Pearce-calibrated) and Poulin and Theil, and each prediction reports its own applicability domain and calibration status. The ensemble that compares the methods refuses to report a dispersion when its members differ in domain or calibration, so a domain mismatch is not presented as uncertainty. Bone, plasma, pancreas and the "other" compartment are refused rather than estimated. The strong-base route adds an input-uncertainty budget for ln Kpu, computed in Sounio, that returns a refusal instead of a value when the blood-to-plasma ratio is inconsistent with the composition tables. The CN step gains `pbpk28_full_cn_step_routed_mut`, which can deliver absorbed drug into the liver (a portal-vein first pass); the existing blood-input functions give the same results as before.
