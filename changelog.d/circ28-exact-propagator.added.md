`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28.sio`: exact propagator for the circ28 kernel (`circ28_exact_prepare`, `circ28_exact_step`, `circ28_exact_step_src`). For constant parameters and piecewise-constant input the step is `x(t+dt) = E x(t) + G u` with `E = exp(A dt)`, built by a uniformisation series and doublings on non-negative matrices, with the elimination and AUC tallies carried exactly. It has no truncation error, preserves positivity at any step length and costs one matrix-vector product per step. The two-stage integrator is unchanged; on coarse grids it can undershoot below zero right after a bolus (reproduced: −8.59 ng/mL per mg), which the propagator does not.
