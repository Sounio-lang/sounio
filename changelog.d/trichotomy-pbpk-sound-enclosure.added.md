New module `epistemic::sound_enclosure` (lie corpus item 1): a heuristic error bound can no longer authorize a decision, because the compiler tells the two apart. `SoundEnclosure` is an opaque interval built only from exact points and outward-rounded `se_add`, `se_sub`, `se_mul` and `se_div` (round-to-nearest followed by the Rump–Zimmermann–Boldo–Melquiond predecessor/successor push), plus the exact `se_absval`, `se_max`, `se_hull` and `se_entire`. `HeuristicErr` can only suggest a refinement. `SafeAuthorization` is minted only by `se_authorize_window`, which panics unless the widened enclosure fits the window. Passing a `HeuristicErr` or an `f64` where a `SoundEnclosure` is required is `error[E009]`; building any of the three structs by literal outside the module is `error[E176]` (`tests/compile-fail/lie01a`–`lie01d`). Known hole, stated in the module: `se_point` trusts that its argument is an exact input.
