New `llm::epistemic_clinical_pbpk`: a three-compartment PBPK decision gate over the Discharge Trichotomy (`formal/lean4/SounioDischargeTrichotomy.lean`). SAFE_AUTHORIZE and NUMERICAL_AUTO_REFINE now require a `SafeAuthorization` minted from `clinical_pbpk_auc_enclosure`, a sound interval for the exact AUC: the last column of the exponential of the augmented matrix `[[B, x0], [0, 0]]·T`, by scaling and squaring with a degree-20 Taylor series and an explicit tail bound, all in outward-rounded interval arithmetic (no matrix inverse, no libm `exp`). At the default parameters the enclosure is 3.6e-11 wide and contains the f64 closed form; RK4 at dt = 0.001 sits 1.47e-6 from its midpoint, the expected trapezoid term. Failed preconditions (nonpositive parameters, T ≤ 0, norm check) return the entire line, which never authorizes. The previous gate compared the RK4 value against the closed-form floating-point budget, which bounds nothing about RK4. Research gate (ADR009); no clinical-validity claim.
