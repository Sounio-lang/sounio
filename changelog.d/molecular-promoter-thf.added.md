`molecular::promoter`: Langmuir constant of a light guest in the small cages of a binary hydrate whose large cages hold a rigid promoter (THF + H₂ in sII), with and without the promoter and with its gradient in the guest parameters; orientation average over a deterministic Halton set. Checked against a C++ oracle on the same quadrature and orientations (`tests/stdlib/molecular/test_promoter_oracle.sio`).
