Public t-test power uses the genuine noncentral-t distribution instead of silently shifting a central t. For a two-sided one-sample test with d=0.8, n=2 and alpha=0.05, power changes from 0.050395432179545306 to 0.078820614542431996. Negative effects retain their direction for right-sided tests. Paired native execution and independent C++23 oracle receipts are recorded in [SIO-167 evidence](../docs/evidence/stdlib-SIO-167/README.md).
