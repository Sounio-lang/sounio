The legacy lean_single x86-64 engine now propagates first-order variance through `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `atan`, `tanh`, `asin`, `acos`, `exp`, `ln`/`log`, and `sqrt`, including method syntax, using `f'(mean)^2 * variance` instead of returning the input variance unchanged (#3551). In particular, `exp(1 ± 0.1)` has first-order variance `0.073891`; Madaros retains its exact lognormal calculation.
