Quantum tape QIR export now rejects gates after the first measurement, repeated measurements of a qubit, malformed tape metadata and non-finite rotation angles before emitting IR. This prevents silent operation reordering and malformed output; the conservative supported subset also rejects late gates on disjoint qubits. Callers can obtain the diagnostic through `tape_qir_base_error`, including `tape_qir: non-finite rotation angle`. QASM export is unchanged.
