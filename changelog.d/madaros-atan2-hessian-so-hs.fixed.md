The second-order (Hessian) rule for the binary `atan2(y, x)` (GUM transfer kind 13) is written against the 64-channel SO_HS Hessian rows (2080 upper-triangle slots per row) that `main` uses since #3588, instead of the 8-channel, 36-slot `[i64; 36]` row it was first written for: `hessian_of(atan2(y, x), j, k)` is now correct for every channel pair 0..63, where the 36-slot form had no slot past channel 7 and read back a structural 0.0 there. The rule takes the argument Hessian H(x) as an SO_HS row (`so_snapshot_expr_hess`), which `fo_apply_transfer_kind` gives back on return. Every second derivative of `tan`, `atan`, `tanh`, `asin`, `acos`, `ln` and `atan2` is checked against Richardson-extrapolated central finite differences of the same builtins, at a generic point and next to each singularity (tan near +-pi/2, asin/acos near +-1, ln near 0, atan2 near the origin and just above its branch cut), including `atan2` differentiated in channels 8 and 9 of ten inputs: `tests/run-pass/math_trans_hessian_fd.sio` (it fails under `SOUNIO_SABOTAGE_HESSIAN_CHAIN=1`).
