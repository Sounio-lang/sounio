`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_kp.sio`: Rodgers–Rowland tissue:plasma partition coefficients for the 13 organs of the circ28 kernel (neutral, acid, base, diprotic base, zwitterion; acidic-phospholipid association constant from the blood:plasma ratio) and the closed-form single scalar that matches a target V_ss. `tests/run-pass/circ28_kp_rodgers_rowland.sio` checks limiting identities and reproduces per-tissue rat Kpu reported for bisoprolol, acebutolol, cefazolin and enoxacin (as transcribed in the code of Utsey et al. 2020) within 3–4 %.
