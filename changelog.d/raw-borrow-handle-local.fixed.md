Fixes the SIO-582 borrow-of-local-struct miscompile: `let hh = &!w` on a local struct binds a slot holding the address of a handle slot, not a payload address, so raw derefs through the alias (`hh as *mut W`, directly, through casts, or through re-aliases) read garbage. A new `is_borrow_handle` bit marks such locals and the raw-deref classifier declines them to the handle-resolving path; loads and stores now round-trip (`45`, store `7` visible through `w`).
