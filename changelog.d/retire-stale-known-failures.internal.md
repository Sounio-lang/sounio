Thirteen stdlib and compiler tests that CI's full suite still carried as known failures — including the ODE, probability, Bayes, fMRI, autodiff and PBPK end-to-end lanes — pass on the current compiler, and the turbofish concrete-type-mismatch compile-fail test is now rejected by both engines for the pinned reason.
