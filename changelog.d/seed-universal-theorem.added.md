The two bootstrap seed loaders are proved correct on every listing, over the Lean model of x86-64 (`formal/Bootstrap/SeedA.lean`, `SeedB.lean`): seed-a exits 0 with output `bs` exactly when the listing describes `bs` in format A, and exits 1 on every listing format A does not describe; likewise seed-b for format B. The proofs are checked by the kernel alone (`decide +kernel`, no `native_decide`) and rest on no axiom beyond propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; `bootstrap/model/seed_proofs.sh` checks them.
