The semaglutide and tirzepatide PBPK28 profiles had a kinetic volume of 21.57 L, about twice the published one, and semaglutide a clearance of 0.077 L/h attributed to Overgaard 2019, which reports 0.0348 L/h. Semaglutide now takes clearance and volume from the Ozempic prescribing information (CL/F 0.05 L/h, V/F 12.5 L, F 0.89: CL 0.0445 L/h, V 11.125 L); tirzepatide keeps its clearance and takes the Vc + Vp of its population model (10.26 L). In both, the tissue Kp are the peptide pattern times one common factor (0.194951 and 0.128280). The single-dose terminal half-life moves from 194 h to 173.5 h for semaglutide (label: about one week) and from 245 h to 116.6 h for tirzepatide (label: about 5 days); the semaglutide steady-state average for 1 mg weekly moves from 68.8 to 119.0 ng/mL (label: 123 ng/mL, not independent of the inputs). `tests/run-pass/pbpk28_peptide_half_life.sio` asserts these and fails on the old profiles. The semaglutide clearance prior in `ep28_semaglutide_priors` becomes 0.0445 L/h with σ = 0.00485 L/h (half the gap between the two sources) in place of 0.077 L/h with a 30% CV; Kp prior means and variances follow the scaling.
