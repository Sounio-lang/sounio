`variance_of` now preserves measurement sensitivities when straight-line mutable totals interleave `measure()` declarations and updates. Independent three-, ten-, and 32-source totals with standard uncertainty 0.1 now produce variances 0.03, 0.10, and 0.32, while repeated uses of a source retain their covariance (#3547). Deterministic initializers no longer consume source channels in straight-line code; the 32-channel limit is unchanged.
