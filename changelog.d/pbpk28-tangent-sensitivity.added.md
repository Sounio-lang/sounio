`darwin_pbpk::sens_pbpk28` carries the first-order GUM sensitivities of the PBPK28 model through the integrator: `ep28_tangent_run` advances, with each θ-step of an IV-bolus run, the tangent ∂x/∂θ_j of every state for the seven epistemic parameters (one extra Schur solve per parameter per step instead of two complete simulations), and returns the four end-point Jacobian columns of `ep28_run` together with a time-resolved combined standard uncertainty u_c(t) for blood and brain. `ep28_run` and its central-difference Jacobian are unchanged; `tests/run-pass/pbpk28_tangent_gum.sio` holds the two routes against each other and against the differentiated mass identity. `ep28_fu_ref` is now public.
