`darwin_pbpk::sens_pbpk28` carries the first-order GUM sensitivities of the PBPK28 model through the integrator: `ep28_tangent_run` (IV bolus) and `ep28_tangent_run_oral` (matrix release, absorption and liver sink: venlafaxine XR, midazolam, haloperidol) advance, with each θ-step, the tangent ∂x/∂θ_j of every state for the seven epistemic parameters (one extra Schur solve per parameter per step instead of two complete simulations), and return the four end-point Jacobian columns of `ep28_run` / `ep28_run_oral` together with a time-resolved combined standard uncertainty u_c(t) for blood and brain. `tests/run-pass/pbpk28_tangent_gum.sio` holds the tangent against the finite-difference Jacobian and against the differentiated mass identity for all five drugs. `ep28_fu_ref` is now public.
