New demo under `demos/aris_bdq/` reimplements the human lung predictions of the bedaquiline PBPK model of Karakitsios et al. (2025) in Sounio, with an independent C++23 oracle and a parity script that requires agreement to a relative 1e-5. The demo also shows the epistemic gate refusing the paper's efficacy claim at a confidence its inputs do not support, a compile-time refusal of a clearance/volume unit swap, and a comparison of a fractal caseum rate with Fickian rivals fitted to the published Table S14 data. No compiler, runtime or standard-library files change.
