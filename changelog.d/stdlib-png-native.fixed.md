PNG export now compiles against the native platform ABI and writes through checked owned file and byte-buffer operations, replacing the undeclared `append_file` call. Public chunk, IHDR and surface entrypoints refuse unsupported lengths and shapes before indexing storage or truncating an output file. PNG call chains declare the required allocation effects. The composed scientific workflow produces a PNG whose pixels, chunk CRCs and zlib Adler checksum are checked by an independent C++23 decoder in the packet-31 receipt.
