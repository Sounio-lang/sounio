`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_drug_semaglutide.sio` and `circ28_drug_tirzepatide.sio`: the two albumin-bound peptides on the circ28 record, with subcutaneous first-order input. The steady-state volume is predicted from interstitial albumin distribution without a scalar (6.84 L and 6.88 L against 6.68 L and 6.73 L from the population models); only the inter-compartmental exchange is fitted. Failures are asserted as measured: the Rodgers–Rowland albumin route over-predicts the volume by 20 %, and the tirzepatide half-life is 152 h against 123 h observed.
