`functional::calculus` (`deriv`, `newton`, `bisect`, `integrate`, `minimize`, `fixed_point`, ...) and the `epistemic::knightian` `pb_apply2_monotone_*` combinators now declare their callback parameters as `fn(..) -> f64 with Mut, Panic, Div`, as SOUNIO-SPEC-06 §6.0 requires. A callback that itself uses `Mut`, `Div` or `Panic` (a Taylor-series `sin`, a ratio `x / y`) was refused with E009; pure callbacks are still accepted by sub-effecting. The bare function-type ratchet drops from 216 (stale) to 172.
