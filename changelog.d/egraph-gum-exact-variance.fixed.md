The GUM-guided e-graph pass (`PRECISION_PRESERVING`) no longer reorders `f64` sums on a rounding artefact. Uncertainty is now propagated as exact integer variances with observation identity: every bracketing of the same independent observations carries the same measurement uncertainty, so extraction keeps the order as written; operands that share an observation, as in `(a+b)+a`, are refused instead of being given the independent-input formula. Before, a per-node `floor(sqrt(·))` made `(a+b)+c` and `a+(b+c)` differ (6 vs 5 nanounits for u = 3, 3, 5, where both are √43 ≈ 6.56), and the pass reassociated the sum, changing its bits, on that difference alone. Programs with an observed three-term epistemic sum may therefore see their `f64` result return to the source evaluation order. Selftests T1157 (updated), T1157b and T1157c.
