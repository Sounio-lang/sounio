`examples/molecular/h2_sii_epistemic.sio`: the H2 content of the sII hydrate as Knowledge for
three water models, with the shared H2 Lennard-Jones covariance and each run's Monte Carlo
error as GUM channels. variance_of a difference between water models keeps only what does not
cancel: the H2 part falls from about 1.0 to 0.02-0.04 H2 per cell, and TIP4P/Ice and TIP5P are
resolved from SPC/E at 300 MPa (12.7 and 5.2 standard uncertainties).
