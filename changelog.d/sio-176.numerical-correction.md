The public viridis, plasma, inferno and magma color functions and default scientific heatmap now use the licensed, pinned 256-entry canonical data, quantized by `floor(255*channel)` with declared listed-bin selection, rather than eight-anchor or polynomial approximations under canonical names. The original algorithms remain available as `*_approx_*` with their prior outputs. Independent C++23 concordance in the [SIO-176 receipts](../tests/stdlib/canonical_palettes/EVIDENCE.md) covers every entry and primary floating channel; the original native witness fails against the canonical oracle with 992 of 1,024 colors differing. This deliberately changes RGB output, including viridis endpoint blue 37→36 under the floor policy, without changing numeric observations or implying statistical calibration.
