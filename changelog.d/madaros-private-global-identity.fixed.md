Two modules' same-named private module globals no longer share one storage slot. Before, a leaf's private `let HK: f64 = 2.5` read the importing module's `HK` and a write to one module's `var` showed through the other, with no diagnostic. A private global that collides with another loaded module's symbol is now renamed to a module-qualified symbol (`HK__m<module>`), as private functions already were, and its initializer, const/exact-f64 facts and E300/E309 refusals follow the rename. Public globals and private globals that another module imports by name keep their shared name, as before.
