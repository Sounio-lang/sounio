The modular compiler resolves call targets after a multi-module merge through a per-name index instead of scanning every function for every call. The answer is the same by construction (each chain holds every function with that name, in order, and the full name is still compared); `SOUNIO_IR_FN_INDEX_VERIFY=1` checks it against the old scan on every lookup and refuses to finish the merge on any disagreement. In the September profile of a self-compile this lookup took 518 of 3,128 seconds.
