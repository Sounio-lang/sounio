Accept numeric type suffixes (`5u8`, `7i32`, `2.5f64`, `0xFFu8`) on both Madaros and `lean_single` engines as specified by ADR-015 row 6 (probes `int_suffix_u8`, `int_suffix_i32`, `flt_suffix`). Out-of-range literals against the suffixed type (`300u8`) are refused with error E276 on both engines (probe `int_suffix_u8_over`). Trailing letters that do not form a valid suffix without boundary continuation continue to split into distinct tokens (`7q` remains `7` and identifier `q`, probe `int_suffix_bad`).
