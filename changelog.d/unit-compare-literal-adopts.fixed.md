Madaros no longer rejects comparing a unit-bearing value against a pure numeric literal with `error[E041]`: the literal adopts the other side's unit in `==`, `!=`, `<`, `<=`, `>` and `>=`, mirroring the D-24 call-argument rule, so `tests/run-pass/unit_same_add.sio` (`total == 500.0` with `total: mg`) compiles again. Comparing against a dimensionless variable, cast, or computed value stays E041. Witnesses: `tests/run-pass/unit_same_add.sio` (run-pass) and `tests/compile-fail/unit_compare_dimensionless_var_sio397.sio` (SIO-397).
