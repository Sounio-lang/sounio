Makes one new lean_single check import-fatal (SIO-60, ADR-020 option (a), probe-only scope): a module-level `let`/`const` bool-vs-scalar mismatch (`let x: i64 = true`) now reports E001 with a direct `TYPECHECK_FAILED` set, in main and imported modules alike (previously bound 0 in silence). Purely additive (77 insertions, 0 deletions); all other diagnostics keep their import-tolerance, since full fatality breaks the self-hosted tree itself. Regression test: `tests/compile-fail/lean_single_import_bool_mismatch.sio`. Analysis of what full fatality would refuse is committed in `docs/compiler/sio60_import_fatal_classification.md`.
