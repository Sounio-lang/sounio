Invalid/unavailable scalar power or sample-size requests now raise runtime `Panic` instead of fabricated zero power or a 1000000 sample-size sentinel. Checked sample-size results distinguish diagnostic status 3 (work exhaustion), 5 (unsupported/ambiguous decision), 6 (cap exhausted) and 7 (unreachable target); successful interior minima expose actual power(n-1)<target<=power(n). The n=2 domain boundary is explicit, and historical shifted-t estimates remain available only through names ending in `shifted_t_approx`.
