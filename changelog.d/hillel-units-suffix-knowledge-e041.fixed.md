Glued unit suffixes such as `140.0<mg_dL>` now parse as branding casts instead of a parse error, so the clinical compile-fail test reports `error[E041]` (unit mismatch) rather than failing in the parser. Knowledge add/sub with mismatched scales also reports `E041`, with a note that Knowledge unit conversion is unsupported, instead of a misleading `E036` about confidence bounds. Witnesses: `tests/run-pass/unit_literal_suffix_values.sio` and `tests/compile-fail/knowledge_unit_scale_mismatch.sio`.
