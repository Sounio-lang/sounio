`hessian_of(pow(x, y))` now includes the exponent second derivative and the mixed partial, not only `∂²/∂x²`. Kind 9 already emitted `∂f/∂x`, `∂²f/∂x²` and first-order `∂f/∂y`; the Hessian loop chained only the base, so an uncertain exponent left `Hxy` and `Hyy` at a confident zero. `tests/run-pass/kl11_pow_mixed_hessian.sio` pins `x=2`, `y=3` at `Hxx=12`, `Hxy=x^(y-1)*(1+y*ln x)` and `Hyy=x^y*(ln x)^2`. Arbitrary user-function bodies remain outside this transfer.
