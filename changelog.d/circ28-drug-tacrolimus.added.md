`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_drug_tacrolimus.sio`: tacrolimus on the circ28 record with local saturable erythrocyte binding (`circ28_nl`). Kp scalar, hepatic and gut intrinsic clearance are fitted to IV volume, IV blood clearance and oral bioavailability; half-life, AUC, blood-to-plasma ratio and dose linearity are validated. The oral peak is under-predicted (8.1 against 28.8 ng/mL) and the test asserts that failure as measured.
