`epistemic::knightian` gains `pb_expanded(value, variance, k, confidence)`, the GUM expanded-uncertainty lift (band `value ± k·σ`, variance moved into the band). `pb_from_knowledge` keeps its zero-gap contract (`fromKnowledge_zero_gap` in `SounioKnightian.lean`). `test_stiff_ode_knightian_mc` now lifts the random input `mu` with `pb_expanded(k = 3)`, subdivides it into 64 sub-boxes to contain interval-Euler wrapping, and runs a 40-step horizon (at 80 steps the band diverges to ±inf). It also checks that the band is not vacuous (tightness 3.16 against a linear prediction of 3). The Klibanoff gap is now checked against the second-order prediction `(α/2)·Var_w` (within 3.8 %) instead of an absolute `1e-3` margin, which a penalty of order `1e-6` could never clear.

The private `pb_sqrt` (used by `pb_expanded` and `pb_dispersion`) now delegates to the range-reduced `math::pure::sqrt`. The old 12-step Newton loop seeded at `y = x` was about 244× off at variances of 1e-12 or 1e12. `test_knightian_expanded_scale` checks the band over v = 0, 1e-12, 0.25 and 1e12, plus negative variance, k = 0 and negative k. It fails on the old helper and passes on the new one.
