The post-merge Madaros self-compile step gets the time and memory it now needs. Since #3046 the gen2 compiler compiles its own source into gen3, and gen2 and gen3 are byte-identical; measured on one node, the gate takes 3 hours and peaks at 55 GiB resident, against a 110 minute job limit and a 48 GiB runner pod. The job limit is raised to 240 minutes and the runner pod memory limit to 72 GiB.
