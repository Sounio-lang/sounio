`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_nl.sio`: saturable erythrocyte binding on circ28 with a local plasma concentration in every blood state (dx/dt = L x + N p(x) + u), integrated by the same two-stage SDIRK with Newton iterations on the exact Jacobian and a fail-closed status. `tests/run-pass/circ28_nl_binding.sio` checks the Jacobian against finite differences, the linear limit against the plain kernel entry by entry, exact non-linear steady states for plasma, renal and hepatic clearance, mass balance, the measured order (1.9) and the difference from the single-ratio approximation of `circ28_blood.sio`.
