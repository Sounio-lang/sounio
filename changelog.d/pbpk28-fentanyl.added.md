PBPK-28 fentanyl (`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/drugs/fentanyl.sio`): fentanyl ported onto PBPK28 from the sedation-weaning study module, which no longer compiles against this stdlib. Adult 70 kg anchor (CL 55 L/h, 93 % hepatic / 7 % renal, PS = 10·Q near flow-limited distribution) and a weight-scaled pediatric variant `pbpk28_params_fentanyl_ped(weight, f_cl)` where `f_cl` multiplies the hepatic clearance only and is an input, not an ontogeny model. The source's naive Σ Kp·V "volume" is replaced by the proper steady-state `V_ss = V_b + Σ V_i·(f_v + (1−f_v)·Kp)` (510.18 L, 7.29 L/kg). `tests/run-pass/darwin_fentanyl_pbpk28.sio` gates the model against values fixed by hand before the run: the clearance closed form, the V_ss closed form and its linear pediatric scaling, the runtime clearance identity (constant infusion, CL = rate/Css to 0.5 %), IV-bolus moment theory (MRT = V_ss/CL and AUC = Dose/CL to 1 %, graded grid to 600 h with a quadrature-convergence gate and a mass identity < 1e-6), and the discriminating pediatric contract that MRT is unchanged when volume and clearance scale together but lengthens by 1/(0.93·f_cl + 0.07) when `f_cl` < 1. Declared, not corrected: the simulated terminal half-life (21.0 h) is 5.8× the 219 min of McClain & Hug 1980 (verified in PubMed), because the source's adipose Kp (12) loads a slow depot; closing it is a calibration decision. Research parity card (ADR009); no clinical-validity claim.
