A local declared `var b: Box<T> = f()` is typed by its annotation, the way a `b: Box<T>` parameter is. Before this, `(*b).field` was resolved by guessing from the field name whenever the initializer was not `Box::new(..)`. With the field-name hash refused, that access became a compile error whenever the field sits at different indices in different structs (`tests/run-pass/annotated_box_local_field_typed.sio`). Separately, once the IR name-id pool overflows, the compiler reports the overflow itself instead of listing every field access that the overflow caused to fail.
