PBPK-28 multidrug28 driver gains CYP1A2 (enzyme code 4; CYP3A4/2D6/2C9/2C19 codes unchanged, so stored scenarios keep their meaning), widening the per-drug enzyme layout from 4 to 5. New `stdlib/darwin_pbpk/ddi/clozapine_fluvoxamine.sio` predicts the fluvoxamine × clozapine interaction without circularity: it does not use the clozapine card's DDI-derived fm, but two independent in-vitro data sets — the CYP partition of clozapine N-demethylation at 5 µM (Olesen & Linnet 2001, DOI 10.1177/00912700122010717) and fluvoxamine's per-isoform Ki (Olesen & Linnet 2000, DOI 10.1097/00004714-200002000-00007) — fed to the driver's own competitive-inhibition factor. The static well-stirred prediction is a 3.0–3.5× AUC rise across hepatic fluvoxamine 4–7 µM, which under-predicts the clinical 5–10× rather than tuning fm to match it; the gap is reported, not closed. `tests/run-pass/darwin_clozapine_fluvoxamine_ddi.sio` checks the driver factor against the closed form through the new CYP1A2 slot and passes on Madaros and lean_single; the five existing multidrug/TR-BDF2/calibration gates pass unchanged on the 5-enzyme layout.
