`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_drug_sirolimus.sio` and `circ28_drug_everolimus.sio`: the two mTOR inhibitors on the circ28 record with local erythrocyte binding and a gut-wall first pass. No intravenous study was found for either, so clearance and volume are apparent and bioavailability is declared. Exposure, steady-state concentrations, half-life and dose linearity pass; the oral peak is under-predicted for both (sirolimus 26 against 67 ng/mL at 10 mg, everolimus 3.7 against 17.8 ng/mL at 2 mg), the sirolimus blood-to-plasma ratio is too low and concentration-dependent, and the ketoconazole interactions exceed what the model can give. All are asserted as measured.
