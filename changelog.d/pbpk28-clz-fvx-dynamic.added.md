PBPK-28 dynamic (flow-limited) fluvoxamine × clozapine follow-up (`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/ddi/clozapine_fluvoxamine_dynamic.sio`): a clozapine victim whose hepatic clearance is the series combination of a delivery conductance (flow + PS) and the CYP intrinsic conductance, so fluvoxamine inhibition is felt through the real PBPK28 transport rather than as a pure CLint ratio. Total clozapine CL is calibrated to 20 L/h, with the uninhibitable 6% carried as an actual direct non-CYP path (1.2 L/h) — the kinetic floor the PR #3454 review flagged as analytic-only in the static harness. Steady state is reached by constant IV infusion (CL = rate/Css). The series-resistance model, fit from two baseline simulations, predicts the full PBPK result to < 0.6%: the delivery ceiling (0.15%), a held-out baseline point (0.002%), the calibration to CL = 20 (0.003%), and the inhibited hepatic clearance at hepatic fluvoxamine 4/5.5/7 µM (0.3–0.6%). Flow limitation blunts the fold-rise by ~13–14% (3.0–3.5× static → 2.6–3.0× dynamic) for clozapine's ~0.22 baseline extraction, widening rather than closing the gap to the clinical 5–10×, so flow limitation is ruled out as the explanation. `tests/run-pass/darwin_clozapine_fluvoxamine_dynamic.sio` gates all of this and passes on Madaros (10 s) and lean_single (23 s).
