PBPK-28 dynamic (flow-limited) fluvoxamine × clozapine follow-up (`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/ddi/clozapine_fluvoxamine_dynamic.sio`): a clozapine victim whose hepatic clearance is the series combination of a delivery conductance (flow + PS) and the CYP intrinsic conductance, so fluvoxamine inhibition is felt through the real PBPK28 transport rather than as a pure CLint ratio. Total clozapine CL is calibrated to 20 L/h, with the uninhibitable 6% carried as an actual direct non-CYP path (1.2 L/h) — the kinetic floor the PR #3454 review flagged as analytic-only in the static harness. Steady state is reached by constant IV infusion (CL = rate/Css). The series-resistance model, fit from two baseline simulations, predicts the full PBPK result to < 0.6%: the delivery ceiling (0.15%), a held-out baseline point (0.002%), the calibration to CL = 20 (0.003%), and the inhibited hepatic clearance at hepatic fluvoxamine 4/5.5/7 µM (0.3–0.6%). Flow limitation blunts the **systemic-clearance** fold-rise by ~13–14% (3.0–3.5× static → 2.6–3.0× dynamic `R_CL`, at fixed oral F) for clozapine's ~0.22 baseline extraction. The **oral-AUC** ratio is a distinct quantity: the same inhibition that cuts systemic CL also raises the hepatic first-pass availability `F_H = 1 − CL_cyp/G`, so `R_oral = (F_inh/F_base)·R_CL` is strictly above `R_CL` (3.09/3.35/3.57× at 4/5.5/7 µM) — the first-pass term and the flow-limitation term act in opposite directions on oral AUC, and `R_CL` alone is not the oral DDI unless `F_inh = F_base` is assumed. Neither the systemic ratio nor the first-pass-carried oral ratio reaches the clinical 5–10× at this model's in-vitro CYP1A2 fraction, so the residual gap is not an artefact of ignoring first-pass; it is left to the next layers (in-vivo CYP1A2 fraction, metabolite inhibition, the perpetrator's own kinetics), documented rather than tuned away. `R_oral` is a model-consistent first-pass correction, not a validated bioavailability (ADR009). `tests/run-pass/darwin_clozapine_fluvoxamine_dynamic.sio` gates all of this and passes on Madaros (12 s) and lean_single.
