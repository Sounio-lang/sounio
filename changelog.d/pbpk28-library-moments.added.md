PBPK-28 tier-A gates for the rest of the library (`tests/run-pass/darwin_pbpk28_library_moments.sio`). Every parameter set that carries its elimination in `cl_central` (14 sets: clozapine, olanzapine, quetiapine, lithium, tacrolimus, everolimus, vancomycin, tirzepatide, risperidone parent and 9-OH, rapamycin, venlafaxine parent and ODV, semaglutide) is run as stored through the multidrug28 driver and checked against values fixed before the run from its own arrays: runtime clearance by constant infusion (CL = rate/Css equals `cl_central`, 0.5 %), IV-bolus moment theory (MRT = V_ss/CL and AUC = Dose/CL, 1 %), quadrature convergence (< 0.2 %) with a residual tail < 1e-6, and the mass identity (< 1e-6). A geometric late time step and an 80·MRT horizon let the fast sets (MRT ~2 h) and the slow peptides (~350 h) share one grid. All 14 pass on Madaros and lean_single with identical numbers (largest MRT deviation 0.24 %, quetiapine, which is trapezoid error at second order). With morphine and fentanyl, 14 of 15 drugs now have an analytic gate; aripiprazole, whose elimination is two hepatic CYP sinks with `cl_central = 0`, is excluded with that reason. The audit report's tier section is updated. These are internal-consistency gates, not validation against published pharmacokinetics (ADR009).
