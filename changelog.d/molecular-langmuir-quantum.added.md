`molecular::langmuir`: the Langmuir constant of a light guest beyond the classical one-site picture — exact quantum centre-of-mass ratio and quantum pair/triple ratios by Feynman–Kac paths, and the charged-guest constant in the Ewald potential of a proton-disordered host. Checked against a C++ oracle on the same random stream and against large independent runs (`tests/stdlib/molecular/test_langmuir_oracle.sio`).
