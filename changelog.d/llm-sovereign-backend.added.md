Added sovereign `stdlib/llm` foundation and ingestion modules: zero-copy multi-shard Safetensors reader (`llm::safetensors_mmap`), fast Qwen3 BPE tokenizer with ChatML formatting and single-digit split (`llm::qwen3_tokenizer`), OS-backed memory mappings (`llm::mmap`), pure-Sounio BF16/F32 kernel generators (`llm::bf16_kernels`), and cross-platform hardware abstraction layer (`llm::hal`) supporting Apple M5 Max Metal zero-copy dispatch and Linux x86-64 CUDA Driver ABI.
