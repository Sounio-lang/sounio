`tests/run-pass/circ28_cmax_causes_a.sio` and `_b.sio`: a diagnostic of why the oral peak stays low after a transit input for tacrolimus, sirolimus, everolimus and venlafaxine. For tacrolimus the cause is the gut-wall Kp multiplied by the V_ss scalar (a gut depot of about 12 h); with the unscaled gut Kp the peak is 29.2 against 28.8 ng/mL. For the other three none of the tested causes closes the gap. No model is changed.
