KL-11 (#1792): add `BinaryOp::OpSub` handling to pure function and constructor transfer classifiers in Madaros lowering (`fo_classify_expr_transfer`, `fo_ctor_classify_leaf`). User functions performing `x - y`, `y - x`, `0.0 - x`, and constant subtractions now preserve first-order sensitivity and second-order Hessian propagation across call boundaries instead of silently dropping variance to zero, pinned by `tests/run-pass/kl11_fo_sub_across_call.sio`.
