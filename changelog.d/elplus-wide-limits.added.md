`stdlib/ontology/elplus.sio`: `elplus_wide_*` entry points with capacity 256 concepts, 16 roles and 32 role chains (was 64 / 8 / 8), validating their inputs and returning a status instead of writing out of range. The legacy entry points keep names, types and layout and now run on the same core; out-of-range inputs to them panic where they used to write past the arrays. `tests/run-pass/elplus_limits_256.sio` covers a 200-concept chain, existential and role-chain derivations with concept ids above 64 and 128, 16 roles with 10 chains, the full 256-concept boundary and every refusal path.
