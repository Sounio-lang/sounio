PBPK-28 morphine (`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/drugs/morphine.sio`): adult 70 kg anchor and a pediatric ontogeny where hepatic clearance is limited by the weakest link of the uptake→conjugation cascade, min(r_UGT2B7, r_OCT1), through the well-stirred model (extraction 0.84 at maturity, 0.28 in the 7-day neonate), with renal clearance on its own GFR maturation curve. Ported from the sedation-weaning study module, which no longer compiles against this stdlib (it imports `core::pbpk28_bridge` and `core::pbpk_params14`, absent here); the port builds `PBPKParams28` directly and drops the unverified ontology IDs. One deliberate deviation from the source: lung PS 0.0 → 175 L/h, applying the module's own PS = 0.5·Q rule (V_ss 151.8 → 157.5 L). `tests/run-pass/darwin_morphine_pbpk28.sio` gates, against values derived before the run: the maturation limit (84 L/h), neonate and infant CL/kg (0.3989 and 1.1131 L/h/kg vs hand 0.3991 and 1.113; the source header's 0.369 and 1.084 predate the GFR curve), the extraction mechanism, the PBPK28 runtime clearance (CL = rate/Css), and the moment identities MRT = V_ss/CL and AUC = Dose/CL (0.17 % and 0.17 %) with a quadrature-convergence gate. A first run failed the AUC gate by 24 % because a uniform 0.05 h trapezoid undersamples the 0.014 h blood turnover; the graded grid fixes it. Declared, not corrected: V_ss is 2.25 L/kg, 32 % below 231 L (Mazoit 2007, as cited by the source) and 16 % above 136 L (Bouwmeester 2004, verified in PubMed), and the ontogeny matures faster than Bouwmeester's 80 %-of-adult-at-6-months. Passes on Madaros and lean_single with identical output.
