Madaros no longer runs numeric kernels 2x to 3x slower than the compiler of 2026-11-09. Two regressions, both fixed without giving up the behaviour they added. (1) The i32 range guard of ADR-031 R1 was a five-op IR window (shift left 32, shift right 32, compare, branch, panic call) after every dynamic i32 `+`, `-` and `*`, about 30 machine instructions per site on the stack-slot emitter; it is now one fused IR operation (`OpAddI32`, `OpSubI32`, `OpMulI32`) whose x86 emitter appends a three-instruction range guard, and arm64 a four-word one. The set of trapping operations and the `i32 +` / `i32 -` / `i32 *` messages are unchanged (they now also name the source location, like the i64 ones). (2) The loop-iteration regions of PR #3564 reset in place at every iteration end through `rep stosq`, even when the iteration allocated nothing; an empty range now skips the string instruction. `scripts/ci/madaros_int_overflow_gate.sh` gains two checks (sections 5 and 6) that fail on the previous compiler, and `tests/perf/madaros_i32_loop_bench.sio` is the benchmark.
