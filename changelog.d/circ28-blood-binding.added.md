`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_blood.sio`: saturable erythrocyte binding on the circ28 kernel (C_blood = (1 − Hct)·C_p + Hct·Bmax·C_p/(Kd + C_p)) through one effective blood:plasma ratio refreshed each step by an implicit midpoint, with a diagnostic for the discrepancy between the local and the effective ratio. `tests/run-pass/circ28_blood_binding.sio` checks the algebra, the linear limit against the plain kernel, exact non-linear steady states for plasma and hepatic clearance, mass balance and the measured order of convergence.
