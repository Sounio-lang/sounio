The subcutaneous-depot input of the semaglutide and tirzepatide PBPK28 scenarios evaluated `exp(-ka·t)` with a 20-term Taylor sum. The sum is accurate while `ka·t` stays below about 4 (the first two weeks) and is wrong after that: at t = 1000 h the tirzepatide rate was negative and the semaglutide rate did not halve every 60 h. Both scenarios now call `math::pure::exp`. `tests/run-pass/pbpk28_sc_depot_release_long_horizon.sio` checks the rate at 24, 168, 504 and 1000 h against the closed form and fails before the change. Within the 30 h and 168 h horizons of the parity cases the two forms agree to rounding; the parity gate is unchanged (50 PASS markers).
