Madaros `println`/`print_f64` of an f64 with magnitude at or above 2^63 printed `9223372036854775808.000000` (SIO-396): the integer part went through `cvttsd2si`, which returns 0x8000000000000000 outside the i64 range. Values from 2^63 up to the largest finite double now print their exact decimal expansion with six fractional zeros, identical to C++23 `printf("%f")`. Values below 2^63 take the unchanged path. `lean_single` was not affected in value (it prints `1.000000e19`); its different notation is unchanged.
