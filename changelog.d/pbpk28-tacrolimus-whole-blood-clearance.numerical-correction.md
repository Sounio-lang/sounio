The PBPK28 tacrolimus oral scenario (`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/scenarios/tacrolimus_oral.sio` and its Node mirror) used a whole-blood clearance of 28 L/h, attributed to Jusko 1995. That paper reports 54 mL/h/kg in liver-transplant patients (3.8 L/h at 70 kg); 28 L/h is not in it. The scenario now takes its disposition from the healthy-volunteer rows of the Prograf prescribing information: CL 0.040 L/h/kg (2.8 L/h), V 1.91 L/kg (tissue Kp scaled by one common factor, 0.484518, to V_ss = 133.7 L), F = 0.18, with ka = 4.5 /h after a 0.39 h lag (Jusko 1995). For the 5 mg fasted dose the prediction moves from AUC(0–72 h) 44.6 ng·h/mL and a 12 h half-life to 252.6 ng·h/mL and 33.4 h, against 243 ± 73 ng·h/mL and 34.8 ± 11.4 h in the label; `tests/run-pass/pbpk28_tacrolimus_healthy_label.sio` asserts both and fails on the old profile. C_max is still under-predicted (15.3 against 29.7 ng/mL), which the test records. Sounio–Node parity for the scenario holds after the change. The PBPK14 tacrolimus model in `drugs/tacrolimus.sio` (`cl_hepatic = 2800`, prior mean 28 L/h) carries the same figure and is not changed here.
