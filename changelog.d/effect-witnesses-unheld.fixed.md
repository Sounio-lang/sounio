`with Foo`, `with NomeQueNaoExiste` and `with NoSuchEffectX` are now refused with `error[E246]` (unknown effect) instead of being dropped in silence. The three spellings were test witnesses for "an unknown effect name must be refused", but `effect_name_is_held` listed them, so the witnesses could never fire; the founder removed them from the hold on 2026-11-22. The other nine held names (`Mod`, `Compute`, `Exp`, `Log`, `Choice`, `Counter`, `Fetch`, `Fail`, `Sampling`) are unchanged. The effect-name closed-list gate now resolves names as builtin, declared (`effect X` / `pub effect X`), held or refused, and requires the accusation witness to be refused with E246. Witness: `tests/compile-fail/effect_unknown_name_witness_unheld.sio`.
