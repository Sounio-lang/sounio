The prebuilt Madaros behind `bin/souc` / `bin/madaros` is rebuilt from `main`, so it now ships today's compiler fixes: stdlib directory-module imports (`use json::*` and others), `str_eq` no longer capturing string equality, E264 for enum payload patterns reached through a reference, `Type::<T>::f()` and name-only generic method matches, `syscall6` borrows of locals, `as_bytes()` and range borrows, `f64` slots of tuple elements in arrays, `Option<T>` call results in `match`, struct `len()` dispatch, `[string; N]` element stores and reads, and the `malformed_ir_registers` diagnostic.
