`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28.sio`: whole-body PBPK kernel with a closed circulation (venous and arterial pools, lung in series, portal drainage of gut, spleen and pancreas into the liver), blood-to-plasma partition, hepatic intrinsic clearance in liver tissue, renal clearance on arterial plasma and oral input into gut tissue, integrated by Crank-Nicolson with one LU factorisation per parameter set. `tests/run-pass/circ28_kernel_identities.sio` checks mass conservation, equilibrium, the AUC identities for plasma, hepatic and renal clearance, and oral F = 1 - E_h.
