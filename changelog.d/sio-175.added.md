Scientific contour programs can prepare a reusable, caller-owned, viewport-bound snapshot and export the same captured integer endpoints and cell/level/branch provenance to Canvas and SVG. The checked API reports exact capacity requirements before commit, preserves the previous capture on failure, owns copied levels and viewport metadata, and permits closing the original field buffers after preparation. Existing saddle, flat-edge, shared-edge, masked-cell and clipping policies are preserved; changing the field, levels or viewport requires preparing again.
