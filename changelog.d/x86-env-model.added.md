Bootstrap: `formal/Bootstrap/X86Env.lean` models the Linux process environment the Sporos interpreter will run in. It covers mapped memory (`mmap`, `mremap`, `munmap`, with zeroed anonymous pages), `open`/`close`, `read`/`write` of many bytes, and the initial stack with `argc`/`argv`. Every choice the kernel is free to make (where a mapping lands, refusing memory, short reads and writes) comes from an oracle, so theorems hold for every kernel. `syscall_wf` proves that every call keeps the mappings page-aligned and disjoint under every oracle, and four witnesses are checked by the kernel.
