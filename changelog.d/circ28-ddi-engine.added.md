`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_ddi.sio`: drug-drug interaction engine on the circ28 kernel for up to three simultaneously simulated drugs — reversible (competitive) inhibition, mechanism-based inactivation and induction of hepatic pathways, with enzyme states advanced by exact exponentials and midpoint splitting. `tests/run-pass/circ28_ddi_identities.sio` checks seven analytic identities (null interaction, IV and oral AUC ratios from the kernel's own extraction formula, fraction metabolised, inactivation and recovery time courses, induction, mass balance).
