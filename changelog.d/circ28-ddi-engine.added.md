`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_ddi.sio`: drug-drug interaction engine on the circ28 kernel for up to three simultaneously simulated drugs — reversible (competitive) inhibition, mechanism-based inactivation and induction of hepatic and gut-wall pathways (separate gut enzyme states and turnover), with enzyme states advanced by exact exponentials and midpoint splitting. `tests/run-pass/circ28_ddi_identities.sio` checks thirteen analytic identities (null interaction, IV and oral AUC ratios from the kernel's own extraction formulas, fraction metabolised, inactivation and recovery time courses, induction, mass balance, and the same for gut-wall pathways, including the oral-above-intravenous contrast).
