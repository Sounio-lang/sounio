`stdlib/darwin_pbpk/circ28_drug.sio`: transit-chain (Erlang) oral input. With `ntr` stages of rate k after a lag, the mass entering gut tissue in a step is `fa·Dose·(S_n(t0) − S_n(t1))`, `S_n` the Erlang survival function, so the input is exact at any step and adds no states. `ntr` belongs to the formulation (`circ28_dose_oral_transit`); `circ28_dose_oral` keeps `ntr = 1` and is unchanged to the bit. `circ28_drug_new` now writes every slot explicitly.
