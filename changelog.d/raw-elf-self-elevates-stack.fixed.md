The raw Madaros compiler ELF self-elevates its stack address-space limit on Linux at startup (getrlimit/setrlimit, capped at the launcher's 512 MiB, best-effort and silent). Check/lower paths recurse deeply enough that the default 8 MiB shell stack SIGSEGV'd (rc=139) on small programs with nested BinOp call args; bin/madaros and the suite wrapper already reserved it via ulimit, but direct ELF invocations ran with whatever the caller had. Non-Linux hosts skip (Linux rlimit numbers); bin/madaros and the suite wrapper report SOUNIO_HOST_OS. Adds run-pass coverage for the SIO-568 repro.
