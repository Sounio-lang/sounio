Bootstrap: `formal/Bootstrap/PDist.lean` adds the probability monad for the physical layer of the x86 model. Distributions are finite and their weights are rational intervals: the silicon's fault rate is a calibration with error bars, not an exact number. `PD.bind_ok` proves that sequencing keeps every weight nonnegative and keeps 1 inside the total-mass interval. `flip` is a single-event upset on one bit with fault probability in an interval (`flip_ok`). `faultFree_bind` proves that the zero-fault branch of a sequence is the sequence of zero-fault branches, which connects the probabilistic semantics to the deterministic proofs already in the tree. Core Lean `Rat` only, without Mathlib or floating point.
