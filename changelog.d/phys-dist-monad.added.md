Bootstrap: `formal/Bootstrap/PDist.lean` adds the probability monad for the physical layer of the x86 model. Distributions are finite, and their weights are exact rationals that sum to 1. The silicon's fault rate is uncertain, so it is an interval `[lo, hi]` on the rate parameter rather than on each weight; every bound is proved for every rate in the interval. Interval weights would widen at every step and make long runs vacuous. `bindF_ok` shows that sequencing keeps a distribution well formed. `faultFree_bindF` shows that the zero-fault branch of a sequence is the sequence of zero-fault branches, which connects the probabilistic semantics to the deterministic proofs already in the tree. `fm_bindF_le'` shows that fault probabilities compose at most additively. `flip` is a single-event upset on one bit at rate `p`, with `flip_ok` and `fm_flip`. The module uses only core Lean `Rat`, with no Mathlib and no floating point.
