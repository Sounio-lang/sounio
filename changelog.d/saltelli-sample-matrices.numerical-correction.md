`epistemic::sobol::saltelli_run` built its two sample matrices from one base-2 radical-inverse sequence: column 0 of matrix A lay in [0, 0.5) and column 0 of matrix B in [0.5, 1), and the remaining columns were strided subsequences of the same sequence. The Saltelli/Jansen estimators assume both matrices uniform and independent, so their output was wrong: for `Y = x0 + 2·x1` (exact S_0 = 0.2, S_1 = 0.8) the run returned S_0 = 1, S_1 = 1, S_T0 = 0.615. The matrices are now rows of a Richtmyer lattice in 2·n_dims dimensions (`frac(k·√p_d)`). At N = 500 and seven inputs the first- and total-order indices of three closed-form models are within 0.05 of the exact values (`tests/run-pass/saltelli_analytic_indices.sio`, which fails before the change). Every index previously produced by `saltelli_run` changes. For the PBPK28 module the rapamycin first-order CL index goes from 0 to 0.850 (total-order 0.892) and semaglutide Σ S_i from 0.966 with S_i(fu) > S_Ti(fu) by 0.39 to 0.820 with the largest S_i − S_Ti excess at 0.010; `pbpk28_sobol_pce` still fails its strict S_i ≤ S_Ti self-test on those sampling-level excesses and stays an expected failure in the dissertation suite gate.
