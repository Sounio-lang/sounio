`tests/run-pass/circ28_distribution_spectrum_a.sio` and `_b.sio`: a diagnostic over eleven drug records — per-organ capacity and equilibration time, the volume reached in 5, 15 and 30 minutes, the simulated central volume against observed two-compartment central volumes, and where the absorbed dose sits at the oral peak. It refutes the hypothesis that the flat oral peaks come from an oversized rapid volume, and shows that a three-compartment transit input reproduces the midazolam and quetiapine peak shape. No model is changed.
