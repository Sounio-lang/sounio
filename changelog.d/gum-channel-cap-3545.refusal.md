Madaros now refuses compilation with E178 when first-order GUM lowering exceeds 32 channels in a function, instead of silently dropping uncertainty sources and understating variance. The same diagnostic refuses `correlate()` on channels beyond the first eight, for both literal and runtime correlation coefficients, instead of silently dropping covariance. These limits apply during lowering, including synthetic residual channels; the checker's separate 256-source noise-symbol limit is unchanged. Fixes #3545.
