BOUNDED CLAIM
For a fixed universe U of four witnesses and quorums Q1,Q2 of size three,
|Q1 intersection Q2| >= |Q1| + |Q2| - |U| = 2. Distinct quorums intersect
in exactly two members; identical quorums intersect in three. If the dishonest
set B has |B| <= 1, then (Q1 intersection Q2) - B is non-empty. Under fixed
membership, verified signatures, and retained non-equivocating honest state,
a current honest witness therefore bridges every anchoring/verification quorum.

Safety and availability are separate. Strict verification fails closed against
a rollback view that would require the sole dishonest member to occupy the full
intersection. Progress requires three protocol-valid matching receipts. One
unavailable member is tolerated only when the other three provide those valid
matching receipts; reachability alone is insufficient.
