BOUNDED CLAIM
Each side is a separate fixed Witness Mesh v1 universe of four members. The host
must independently replay and verify at least three current signed statuses and
frame 9014 under the old membership, and separately do the same under the new
membership. Frame 9015 then requires both exact 3-of-4 summaries, distinct
membership and state-root digests, identical domain/event-count/journal-head
coordinates, positive side-specific anchor sequences, distinct nonzero boundary
certificate digests, adjacent epochs, and a predecessor digest of the correct
genesis/non-genesis shape.

The native old and new quorum tokens are linear, carry their admitted epoch, and
can only be minted from verified=1, required=3, members=4 summaries under the
explicit host-trust effect. The join refuses caller attempts to rebind those
tokens to different epochs. Its private linear receipt transports both compact
authority summaries and is consumed once. The persisted OCaml receipt separately
retains the complete certificate bytes, signed status bytes, membership/root
identities, shared checkpoint, and predecessor path; cold verification reloads
and verifies those retained objects rather than trusting receipt assertions.

Frame 9015 checks only predecessor digest shape. The OCaml verifier resolves the
digest, recursively verifies each retained receipt and both frame-9014 sides,
and binds previous.to_epoch, previous.new_membership, previous.new_root, event
monotonicity, and the preceding journal head. Frame 9015 is necessary but not
sufficient. V0 admits from_epoch 1 through 64 and active epochs through 65;
longer histories require a future compaction or roll-forward authority.

ATTACKS AND CAUSAL CONTROLS
Both authority-laundering directions are executed: two valid new statuses with a
complete old side refuse, and two valid old statuses with a complete new side
refuse. Each of the seven named frame rules has an exact same-frame control that
is refused in production and admitted only when that one named rule is replaced
by true. The certificate-reuse join is refused in production and admitted when
only witness_epoch_certificates_are_bound is sabotaged. Separate controls prove
linear quorum reuse E039, private receipt construction E176, epoch rebinding
refusal, and unverified 3-of-4 summary refusal.

Preparation atomically writes and re-verifies one deterministic handoff receipt
before activation. Activation atomically replaces a separate canonical pointer.
A crash after prepared-receipt fsync leaves the prior authority active and retry
reuses the receipt. A crash after pointer replacement returns the same transition
idempotently. Conflicting prepared or active state refuses instead of overwriting.
