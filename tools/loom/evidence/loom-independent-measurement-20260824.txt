MECHANISM

The observer derives a four-field predecessor vector from retained generation
artifacts rather than the signer's receipt: generation identity, generation
fingerprint, semantic journal head, and Guardian journal head. It signs that
vector together with the raw journal and descriptor digests. The OCaml host
separately verifies the signer receipt, observer signature, raw hash chains,
canonical attestation, and recomputed measurement. It deliberately does not
compare the signer-declared vector with the measured vector.

The host sends both vectors to the native Sounio pre-spawn adapter. Sounio
creates private VerifiedMeasurementAgreement only when all four compact tokens
agree, after it has also created disjoint-principal evidence. Decision facts and
measured facts are separate nominal types. Agreement is a private constructor.

TREATMENT

a canonical receipt before observation. The observer then derives and signs the
unchanged semantic head from the retained raw journal. Native Sounio refuses the
resulting disagreement before start_command. No successor receipt is created.

BOUNDARIES AND RESIDUAL RISKS

The observer is independent from the signer receipt, not from the authority
that writes the journals at event time. An authority that writes a
consistent-but-false journal and signs a matching receipt can still produce
agreement. Journal-write custody is therefore the next load-bearing boundary.

Principal and fact values enter Sounio as compact positive 60-bit tokens. A
principal-token collision fails closed as false disjointness refusal. A
fact-token collision has the opposite direction: a genuinely differing field
can appear equal and false-admit the underlying disagreement, approximately
2^-60 for the one-field mismatch exercised here. This receipt therefore makes
no full-256-bit cryptographic equality claim.

This bounded witness does not establish organizational, process, hardware, or
trust-domain independence; collusion resistance; trusted key or journal-write
custody; revocation; Byzantine resistance; distributed consensus; exactly-once
external effects; PL/CS novelty; or priority.
