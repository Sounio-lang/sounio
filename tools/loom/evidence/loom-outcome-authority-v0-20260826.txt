ESTABLISHED BOUNDARY
--------------------

For a contingent policy compiled with the complete signed authority
configuration inside one filesystem authority:

1. Compile commits pairwise-distinct policy-owner, measurement-principal, and
   classifier-principal identities, two distinct Ed25519 public-key IDs, and a
   classifier-specification SHA-256 digest.
2. The measurement receipt signs exact measurement bytes plus the replayed
   policy ID, selected-policy digest, current path and action, policy owner,
   generation, exact journal head, measurement principal and key ID, and a
   carried nonce.
3. The classification receipt signs the complete measurement-receipt digest,
   measurement digest and nonce, the same replayed machine coordinate, its
   committed classifier identity and key ID, the precommitted classifier-spec
   digest, the recomputed current nominal partition, and one outcome ID.
4. OCaml verifies both canonical receipts, both committed public keys, and both
   Ed25519 signatures. Native Sounio frame 9012 independently checks named
   semantic role, measurement, classification, and carried-nonce rules.
   Native frame 9011 independently checks the contingent branch route.
5. The exclusive machine.lock remains held across replay, receipt verification,
   successor-resource validation, one append containing both receipts and the
   transition, fsync, and post-append replay.
6. The exact pre-append journal head is the local compare-and-swap coordinate.
   Any intervening event stales the receipts even when the policy cursor is
   unchanged. Two processes presenting the same valid receipt pair produce
   exactly one winner.
7. Apache Arrow remains a verified-derived projection. It never becomes receipt
   or journal authority.

BOUNDED INPUTS AND CANONICALIZATION
-----------------------------------

  measurement bytes: at most 16 MiB
  canonical measurement receipt: at most 16 KiB
  canonical classification receipt: at most 16 KiB
  committed public-key PEM: at most 8 KiB each
  signature encoding: canonical padded Base64 only
  principal and nonce atoms: existing 256-byte epistemic atom bound

Crossing a bound refuses before signing, classification, or append. Receipt
parsers require the exact canonical serialization, exact field set/order, valid
nonzero SHA-256 domains, and a 64-byte Ed25519 signature after canonical Base64
decoding.
