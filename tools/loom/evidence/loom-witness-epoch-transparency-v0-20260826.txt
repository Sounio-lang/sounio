CLAIM BOUNDARY

The bounded mechanism joins a verified frame-9015 handoff with a signed external
append-only journal, complete RFC6962-style materialized-prefix recomputation,
and a fresh 3-of-4 checkpoint from one fixed four-member witness council. A
strong active read requires the handoff to be the journal's latest leaf. Under
the declared n=4, q=3, f<=1 assumption, it refuses rollback below the latest
quorum-witnessed epoch while the operator and at least three current witness
statuses are reachable.

The cross-node gate establishes physical separation for this run: one client,
one operator with node-local state, and four witness services ran on five
distinct Slurm hosts. It does not establish trusted time, freeze detection,
availability, progress through a partition, recovery of missing log bytes,
consensus, state-machine replication, safety with two dishonest witnesses, or
protection when the operator and at least two witnesses collude.
