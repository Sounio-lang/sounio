Claim under test
----------------

The coordinator previously keyed a wake receipt by message ID and lane-stable
endpoint ID. If a recipient generation received a wake and died before ACK, its
successor reused that endpoint ID and the durable bus incorrectly suppressed
the pending wake.

The repaired rule keys delivery by message ID, endpoint ID, and verified process
generation. Delivery is at least once across generations, deduplicated within a
generation, and suppressed for every later generation after the recipient's
durable ACK. A wake receipt proves transport delivery only. It does not prove
hook execution, agent response, or exactly-once execution of message effects.

Crash witness
-------------

`scripts/ci/sounio_loom_selftest.sh` starts a Loom-owned coordination endpoint,
delivers one directed request, retries it in the same generation, and observes
deduplication. It then sends SIGKILL to kernel, Guardian, and harness without
acknowledging the message. A distinct successor generation receives the same
message and deduplicates its own retry. The bus exposes two wake receipts with
different generation IDs. After recipient ACK, a third complete generation is
started; retry is refused as acknowledged, the third PTY contains no message ID,
and the durable wake count remains two.
