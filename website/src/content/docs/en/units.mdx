# Units of Measure

Status: Sounio 3.0 (pre-release).

On the committed prebuilt, a length divided by a time runs, and a length added to a time is `error[E041]`. The programs, the printed line `10.438413`, and the diagnostic are on [Units and Uncertainty](/learn/language/units-and-uncertainty/). Effects in the signature are a separate page: [the effect system](/learn/effects/).

The spelling `Quantity[f64, m/s]` and a trailing `@` is not the path those programs use. `examples/darwin_pbpk/simulation_demo.sio` failed to parse on that prebuilt at `@` (line 80, 20 November 2026). Do not copy that spelling from an old note.

## Where the checker lives

- `self-hosted/check/units.sio` is the unit checker in the self-hosted tree.
- The refusal witness for kilometres plus metres is `tests/compile-fail/diagnostic_e041_unit_meaning.sio`.
- `docs/compiler/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` is the open ledger. KL-15: no `Knowledge<f128>`.
